# Reading

## T-R1 · Completar palabras (8)

En cada palabra se muestran las primeras letras; completa el resto.

1. ach____ (logro)
2. adv_____ (ventaja)
3. circ_____ (circunstancia)
4. dest_______ (destino)
5. sugg___ (sugerir)
6. ordin____ (ordinario)
7. regu___ (regular)
8. tempo_______ (temporal)

## T-R2 · Leer en la vida diaria (4)

> **Lakeside Gym · New timetable from 1 March.** Weekday classes start at 6:30 a.m. Yoga is now on Thursdays instead of Tuesdays. The pool is closed on Mondays for maintenance. Members must book classes online at least two hours in advance.

1. When does yoga take place now? A) Tuesdays B) Thursdays C) Mondays
2. When is the pool closed? A) Mondays B) Fridays C) at weekends
3. How early must members book classes? A) two hours in advance B) on the same day C) a week in advance
4. What changes from 1 March? A) the prices B) the timetable C) the location

## T-R3 · Pasaje académico (5)

> Lichens are not single organisms but partnerships between a fungus and an alga or cyanobacterium. The fungus provides structure and protection, while the photosynthetic partner supplies sugars. Because lichens can survive in harsh environments such as bare rock and polar regions, they are often among the first life forms to colonise new land. Scientists also use lichens as indicators of air quality, since many species are sensitive to pollution and disappear from polluted areas.

1. What is a lichen? A) a single plant B) a partnership between a fungus and an alga or cyanobacterium C) a type of rock
2. What does the photosynthetic partner provide? A) structure B) protection C) sugars
3. Why are lichens often among the first life forms on new land? A) They need a lot of water. B) They survive harsh environments. C) They grow only in cities.
4. How do scientists use lichens? A) to make medicine B) as building materials C) as indicators of air quality
5. The word "sensitive" is closest in meaning to: A) easily affected B) very large C) rarely found

# Listening

> El texto se lee en esta etapa. Cuando se apruebe el audio, cada tarea se escucha una vez, como en el examen.

## T-L1 · Elegir una respuesta (6)

1. "Could you tell me where the library is?" A) It's on the second floor. B) I borrowed two books. C) Libraries are quiet.
2. "Do you want to come to the match?" A) Matches are long. B) I play football, actually. C) I'd love to, what time?
3. "Why are you so tired?" A) I went to bed early. B) I was up all night working on my essay. C) Tiredness is common.
4. "Have you spoken to the teacher about the grade?" A) Grades are important. B) Yes, she agreed to look at it again. C) The teacher is tall.
5. "Is it okay if I sit here?" A) Sitting is good for you. B) I'm sitting down. C) Of course, go ahead.
6. "We're out of milk." A) I'll pick some up on the way home. B) Milk is white. C) I drink milk every morning.

## T-L2 · Conversación (4)

> **W:** Excuse me, I think I've been charged twice for my hotel room.
> **M:** Let me check your booking. I can see two payments, one from Monday and one from Tuesday.
> **W:** The second one was a mistake. I only paid once.
> **M:** I'll refund it. It will appear in three to five days.

1. What is the woman's problem? A) She lost her room key. B) She was charged twice. C) The room is dirty.
2. What does the man do first? A) He checks the booking. B) He cancels the booking. C) He changes the room.
3. What will the man do? A) call the police B) charge her more C) refund the payment
4. When will the money appear? A) today B) in three to five days C) next month

## T-L3 · Anuncio (3)

> Good afternoon, passengers. The 3:15 ferry to the island is delayed by forty minutes because of strong winds. Please wait in the terminal building, where hot drinks are available. Passengers with tickets for the 4:00 ferry should not be affected.

1. Which ferry is delayed? A) the 3:15 B) the 4:00 C) the 5:15
2. Why is the ferry delayed? A) engine problems B) strong winds C) a crowd of passengers
3. Where should passengers wait? A) in the terminal building B) on the pier C) on the ferry

## T-L4 · Charla académica (4)

> Today I want to talk about why teenagers need more sleep than adults. During puberty, the body's internal clock shifts later, so many teenagers naturally feel awake at night and tired in the morning. Schools that start very early therefore make it harder for students to concentrate. Some districts have pushed back start times, and early results show better attendance and grades. Parents can help by keeping consistent bedtimes, even at weekends.

1. Why do teenagers need more sleep? A) They study more. B) Their body clock shifts later during puberty. C) They exercise less.
2. What effect do very early start times have? A) They make concentration harder. B) They help students concentrate. C) They reduce attendance.
3. What have some districts done? A) increased homework B) pushed back start times C) closed schools
4. What can parents do to help? A) keep bedtimes consistent B) give more homework C) buy new clocks

# Writing

## T-W1 · Construir una oración (5)

Ordena las palabras para formar una oración correcta, con mayúscula y puntuación.

1. yesterday / the / team / finished / the / project
2. she / has / worked / here / for / five / years
3. if / you / study / harder / you / will / pass
4. the / museum / we / visited / was / closed
5. must / you / bring / your / ID / to / the / exam

## T-W2 · Escribir un correo (1)

> Faltaste a una clase. Escribe un correo a tu profesora para pedir los apuntes y proponer cuándo puedes reunirte con ella para ponerte al día. **Extensión:** entre 80 y 120 palabras (criterio de GRAVITY; la extensión oficial no se confirmó en la especificación).

## T-W3 · Discusión académica (1)

> **Profesor:** ¿Deberían las universidades dar libros de texto gratis a todos los estudiantes? Escribe tu opinión en al menos 100 palabras y responde a uno de tus compañeros.
> **Ali:** It would reduce debt, but publishers would lose money.
> **Mei:** Libraries already share books, so universities could expand that idea.

# Speaking

## T-S1 · Escuchar y repetir (5)

Repite la frase tal como la escuchas. Se califica la pronunciación, el ritmo y la exactitud.

1. The meeting has been moved to Thursday afternoon.
2. Could you tell me where the nearest post office is?
3. I've been waiting for almost an hour.
4. She doesn't usually take the bus to work.
5. If the weather improves, we might go hiking.

## T-S2 · Entrevista (3)

Responde con claridad y con detalles. Tienes unos 30 segundos por respuesta.

1. What is your favourite way to relax?
2. Describe a place you would like to visit and explain why.
3. Do you think it is better to study online or in person? Why?

# Clave y retroalimentación

| Sesión | Respuestas | Regla por error |
|---|---|---|
| T-R1 | 1 achieve · 2 advantage · 3 circumstance · 4 destination · 5 suggest · 6 ordinary · 7 regular · 8 temporary | Revisa la ortografía completa de cada palabra; la forma escrita depende del sufijo (*-ment*, *-tion*, *-ary*). |
| T-R2 | 1 B · 2 A · 3 A · 4 B | Un aviso se lee buscando el cambio exacto ("instead of", "from 1 March"). Cada pregunta corresponde a una línea del texto. |
| T-R3 | 1 B · 2 C · 3 B · 4 C · 5 A | Una definición se encuentra en la primera frase del pasaje. El sinónimo de una palabra se decide por el contexto de la oración, no por la palabra más parecida. |
| T-L1 | 1 A · 2 C · 3 B · 4 B · 5 C · 6 A | Una respuesta correcta responde a la pregunta o la frase, no solo repite una palabra. |
| T-L2 | 1 B · 2 A · 3 C · 4 B | Lo que se pregunta sobre "first" o "will" es una acción concreta; revisa la frase exacta del hablante. |
| T-L3 | 1 A · 2 B · 3 A | Un anuncio da la información exacta. Las opciones con datos parecidos son distractores. |
| T-L4 | 1 B · 2 A · 3 B · 4 A | "Therefore" marca una consecuencia; úsalo para identificar la relación entre ideas. |
| T-W1 | "The team finished the project yesterday." · "She has worked here for five years." · "If you study harder, you will pass." · "The museum we visited was closed." · "You must bring your ID to the exam." | Revisa el tiempo verbal (*has worked* para algo que sigue vigente) y el orden de la oración condicional. |
| T-W2 y T-W3 | Rúbrica de `rubrica-evaluacion.md` | Un correo necesita saludo, motivo claro y propuesta concreta. La discusión necesita tu opinión y una respuesta a un compañero. |
| T-S1 | Rúbrica | Revisa el acento de la palabra principal y el ritmo; no omitas palabras como "the" o "to". |
| T-S2 | Rúbrica | Responde con detalles: ejemplo, razón y una conclusión breve. |

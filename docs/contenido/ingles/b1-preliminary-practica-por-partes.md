## Reading

### B1-1 · Parte 1 · Textos cortos (5 preguntas, 3 opciones)

1. *Sign:* "The swimming pool is closed for cleaning. It will reopen on Friday." When will the pool reopen? A) Thursday B) Friday C) Monday
2. *Text:* "Hi Sam, the meeting is in Room 4 today, not Room 2." Where is the meeting? A) Room 4 B) Room 2 C) Room 6
3. *Notice:* "Please do not feed the ducks. Bread is bad for them." What should visitors not do? A) feed the ducks B) walk by the lake C) take photos
4. *Email:* "Your order will arrive on Tuesday morning. If you are out, leave a note for the driver." What should you do if you are out? A) wait at home B) call the shop C) leave a note for the driver
5. *Notice:* "Books must be returned within two weeks. Late books cost 50 pence per day." How much does a late book cost? A) 50 pence B) 5 pounds C) 2 pounds

### B1-2 · Parte 2 · Relacionar personas con textos (4 personas, 6 textos)

Lee los textos A a F. Relaciona cada persona (1 a 4) con el texto adecuado. Hay dos textos que no se usan.

- **A.** A jogging group meets at the park gates every Saturday at eight.
- **B.** The museum offers free guided tours for families on Sundays.
- **C.** A new Italian restaurant has opened, with a menu for children.
- **D.** The library needs volunteers to help with the reading club.
- **E.** The cinema has reduced prices for students on Wednesdays.
- **F.** A cooking course for beginners starts next month at the college.

1. Maria wants to learn to cook.
2. Jon wants to take his children somewhere on the weekend.
3. Priya is a student and likes going to the cinema.
4. Ahmed enjoys reading to children.

### B1-3 · Parte 3 · Opción múltiple (4 preguntas, 4 opciones)

> Last week I finally tried rock climbing. The instructor was patient and explained every step before we started. I was scared at first, but the climb was easier than I expected. Now I'm planning to go again, this time with my brother.

1. How did the writer feel at first? A) bored B) scared C) confident D) tired
2. How was the climb? A) harder than expected B) the same as before C) easier than expected D) impossible
3. What was the instructor like? A) rude B) patient C) late D) silent
4. Who will the writer go with next time? A) alone B) with friends C) with a teacher D) with her brother

### B1-4 · Parte 4 · Insertar oraciones (4 huecos, 5 oraciones)

Elige la oración correcta (A a E) para cada hueco. Sobra una oración.

> Cycling is becoming more popular in cities. **(1)** ___ Cycling is cheap, healthy and good for the environment. **(2)** ___ Start with short trips on quiet streets and build up slowly. **(3)** ___ Many cities have now built special lanes for bikes, which makes it safer. **(4)** ___

A. Some people, however, are still afraid of traffic.
B. Riding a bike is easier than most people think.
C. Most importantly, you should wear a helmet.
D. Good planning makes the first trip much easier.
E. Finally, remember to check your brakes regularly.

### B1-5 · Parte 5 · Vocabulario, opción múltiple (5 preguntas, 4 opciones)

1. The concert was so ___ that I didn't want it to end. A) boring B) thrilling C) tiring D) empty
2. Please ___ off your phone before the film starts. A) turn B) take C) put D) switch
3. She ___ her keys and had to wait outside. A) forgot B) lost C) missed D) left
4. The meeting was ___ until next Monday. A) put off B) turned up C) gone on D) cut down
5. I'm ___ of waiting for the bus every morning. A) fed up B) fond C) keen D) afraid

### B1-6 · Parte 6 · Completar con una palabra (5 huecos)

> Escribe **una** palabra para cada hueco.

> Hello Sam, I'm writing **(1)** ___ you about the party. It starts at seven **(2)** ___ Saturday and finishes at midnight. Please let me **(3)** ___ whether you can come. **(4)** ___ you can bring some music, that would be great. I'm really **(5)** ___ forward to seeing you.

## Writing

### B1-7 · Parte 1 · Correo de cuatro puntos (unas 100 palabras)

> **From:** Tom · **Subject:** Surprise party!
> I'm planning a surprise birthday party for Lisa next Saturday. Could you **(1) help me choose a cake**? Please **(2) let me know if you can bring some music**. **(3) Tell me what time would suit you to arrive**. And **(4) suggest a good place to hold it**.

Responde a los cuatro puntos. **Criterios:** los cuatro puntos cubiertos; tono adecuado; gramática y vocabulario del nivel; unas 100 palabras.

### B1-8 · Parte 2 · Artículo o historia (unas 100 palabras)

Elige una:
- **Artículo** para la revista de la escuela: un deporte o actividad que disfrutas, por qué te gusta y qué consejo darías a alguien que quiera empezar.
- **Historia** que empiece así: *"When the phone rang at midnight, I already knew something was wrong."*

## Listening

> El texto se lee en esta etapa. Cuando se apruebe el audio, cada parte se escucha dos veces.

### B1-9 · Parte 1 · Opción múltiple con dibujos (3 preguntas, 3 opciones)

1. **W:** Where did you leave the umbrella? **M:** Under the table near the door. → A) under the table near the door B) in the car C) in the kitchen
2. **W:** What time will we meet? **M:** Quarter past eight at the station. → A) quarter to eight B) quarter past eight C) eight
3. **W:** Is the shop open on Sundays? **M:** No, only Monday to Saturday. → A) yes, always B) Sundays only C) no, Monday to Saturday only

### B1-10 · Parte 2 · Frases incompletas (4 huecos)

> We're going to cycle to the lake on Saturday. We'll leave from the school car park at nine. Please bring a water bottle and a waterproof jacket, because it may rain. Lunch will be at the café by the water.

1. They will cycle to the ________.
2. They leave from the school ________ at nine.
3. Bring a ________ bottle.
4. Lunch will be at the ________.

### B1-11 · Parte 3 · Opción múltiple con hablantes (3 hablantes, 4 opciones)

> Para cada hablante (1 a 3), elige la razón de la lista (A a D). Sobra una opción.

**Opciones:** A) she is changing her job · B) she is moving house · C) she wants to study abroad · D) she had a bad experience

1. "I'm packing boxes all week, because the flat is too small now."
2. "I've been accepted onto a course in Canada, so I'll go next year."
3. "I was ill for a week, and I'm just starting to feel normal again."

### B1-12 · Parte 4 · Entrevista (4 preguntas, 3 opciones)

> **Interviewer:** Ana, how did you learn to paint?
> **Ana:** Mainly by watching videos online. I made so many mistakes at first, but I didn't give up.
> **Interviewer:** What do you enjoy most?
> **Ana:** Painting outside. The light changes all the time, so every picture is different.
> **Interviewer:** Do you sell your work?
> **Ana:** Only a few paintings, to friends. I don't want to make painting a job yet.
> **Interviewer:** What advice would you give beginners?
> **Ana:** Don't worry about mistakes. Everyone makes them.

1. How did Ana learn to paint? A) by watching videos online B) at school C) from her mother
2. What does she enjoy most? A) painting in the studio B) selling paintings C) painting outside
3. Does she sell her work? A) only a few, to friends B) yes, all of it C) no, never
4. What advice does she give? A) practise every day B) don't worry about mistakes C) buy expensive paints

## Speaking

> Se practica en voz alta, con grabación, en pareja. Se califica con la rúbrica de `rubrica-evaluacion.md`.

### B1-13 · Parte 1 · Conversación (4 preguntas)

- Where do you live, and what do you like about it?
- What do you usually do at the weekend?
- Do you prefer reading or watching films? Why?
- What was the last present you gave someone?

### B1-14 · Parte 2 · Turno largo con fotos (1 minuto de preparación, hasta 2 minutos)

Describe dos fotos. **Foto A:** una familia caminando por un sendero de montaña con mochilas. **Foto B:** un grupo de amigos cocinando juntos en una cocina grande. Compara lo que hacen las personas y di qué actividad prefieres.

### B1-15 · Parte 3 · Tarea colaborativa (2 a 3 minutos)

La escuela quiere organizar un evento para recaudar fondos: una noche de películas, un concurso de talentos o un día deportivo. Decidan juntos cuál conviene más y por qué.

### B1-16 · Parte 4 · Discusión (3 preguntas)

- Do you think it's important for children to learn a musical instrument? Why?
- How do people in your country usually celebrate birthdays?
- Is it better to study alone or in a group? Why?

# Clave y retroalimentación

| Sesión | Respuestas | Regla por error |
|---|---|---|
| B1-1 | 1 B · 2 A · 3 A · 4 C · 5 A | Lee el dato exacto que pide la pregunta (el día, el lugar, el precio). Un texto corto suele tener la respuesta en una sola frase. |
| B1-2 | 1 F · 2 B · 3 E · 4 D | Busca la palabra clave de la persona (*learn to cook*, *children*) y su equivalente en el texto. Los textos sobrantes son distractores. |
| B1-3 | 1 B · 2 C · 3 B · 4 D | Una pregunta sobre cómo se sintió alguien se responde con la palabra exacta del texto ("scared"). |
| B1-4 | 1 B · 2 D · 3 A · 4 E (sobra C) | Una oración encaja por lo que viene antes y después: *however* marca un contraste, *finally* cierra. |
| B1-5 | 1 B · 2 D · 3 B · 4 A · 5 A | Una palabra se elige por la combinación con la palabra siguiente: *switch off*, *put off*, *fed up of*. Revisa la colocación, no solo el significado. |
| B1-6 | 1 to · 2 on · 3 know · 4 If · 5 looking | Una preposición va después de *writing to* (a) y de un día (*on Saturday*). *Let me know* es la forma fija. *Looking forward to* es una expresión fija. |
| B1-7 y B1-8 | Rúbrica | Revisa que cubras cada punto del correo; en la historia, el tiempo verbal del pasado debe ser coherente. |
| B1-9 | 1 A · 2 B · 3 C | Escucha la respuesta del segundo hablante, no solo la pregunta. "Under the table" responde a dónde. |
| B1-10 | 1 lake · 2 car park · 3 water · 4 café | Responde con la palabra exacta que escuchas. |
| B1-11 | 1 B · 2 C · 3 D (sobra A) | Lo que dice el hablante se relaciona con la razón de la lista aunque no la nombre ("packing boxes" indica mudanza). |
| B1-12 | 1 A · 2 C · 3 A · 4 B | La respuesta correcta es lo que la persona dice explícitamente; las opciones incorrectas invierten lo que dijo. |
| B1-13 a B1-16 | Rúbrica | Revisa fluidez, vocabulario, pronunciación y que respondas con detalles, no con una sola palabra. |

## Umbrales por sesión

- Listening (B1-9 a B1-12): 80 % de cada parte.
- Writing y Speaking (B1-7, B1-8, B1-13 a B1-16): rúbrica de `rubrica-evaluacion.md`, 80 % por criterio.

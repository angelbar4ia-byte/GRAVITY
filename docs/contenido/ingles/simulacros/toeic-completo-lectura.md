## Parte 5 · Oraciones incompletas (preguntas 101 a 130)

## Parte 6 · Textos con huecos (preguntas 131 a 146)

Lee cada texto. Elige la opción que completa cada hueco.

**Texto 1 · Correo a todo el personal (131 a 134)**
> **To:** All staff
> **Subject:** Parking changes
>
> Starting Monday, the north parking lot will be closed for repairs. Employees who usually park there **(131)** ____ use the east lot instead. Shuttle buses will run every fifteen minutes from the east lot to the main entrance. **(132)** ____, please allow extra time in the morning. We apologize for any inconvenience and thank you for your patience. **(133)** ____ questions, contact Facilities at extension 220. **(134)** ____

131. A) should B) will C) might D) have to → **A**
132. A) Therefore B) However C) Although D) Meanwhile → **A**
133. A) If you have B) Have you C) Having D) You have → **A**
134. A) The repairs will take about three weeks. B) We look forward to hearing from you. C) Please do not park in the north lot. D) Lunch is served at noon. → **C**

**Texto 2 · Anuncio de un curso (135 a 138)**
> Join our new course on customer service. The course is designed for staff who want to improve their communication skills. **(135)** ____ the sessions take place on Tuesday evenings, participants can attend online as well. The course lasts six weeks, **(136)** ____ each session includes practical exercises. Those who complete the course will receive a certificate. **(137)** ____ registration closes on the first of March. **(138)** ____ at the human resources office or by email.

135. A) Although B) Because C) Unless D) Until → **A**
136. A) so B) but C) or D) yet → **A**
137. A) Please register early, because B) Registration is free, C) The course starts in April, D) Our office is downtown → **A**
138. A) Register B) Registering C) Registered D) Registers → **A**

**Texto 3 · Carta de un cliente (139 a 142)**
> Dear Mr. Ortiz, I am writing to thank you for the excellent service my team received last week. The installation was completed on time, and the technicians were very professional. **(139)** ____ we have had problems with other suppliers, this experience was different. **(140)** ____ we would like to order additional equipment next month. **(141)** ____ send us a quotation by Friday, please? **(142)** ____ Sincerely, Laura Vega

139. A) Unlike B) During C) Despite D) Unless → **A**
140. A) Therefore B) However C) Also D) Yet → **C**
141. A) Could you B) Did you C) Would you D) Do you → **A**
142. A) We look forward to your reply. B) The equipment is expensive. C) Our office is closed. D) The weather is cold. → **A**

**Texto 4 · Mensaje interno sobre un proyecto (143 a 146)**
> To: Project team. The deadline for the Morgan report has been moved to Friday. **(143)** ____ the team agrees on the final numbers by Wednesday, we can finish the layout on Thursday. Please send your sections to Anita **(144)** ____ noon on Tuesday. The client has asked for a summary, **(145)** ____ we will prepare one. **(146)** ____ questions, call me directly.

143. A) If B) Unless C) Although D) Because → **A**
144. A) by B) until C) since D) at → **A**
145. A) so B) but C) yet D) or → **A**
146. A) Any B) Some C) All D) Each → **A**

> Nota de calidad: en la Parte 6 hay que revisar que la forma "Any" (146) y el hueco 144 ("by noon") tengan una sola respuesta. Se marca para revisión antes de usar.

## Parte 7 · Lectura de textos (preguntas 147 a 200)

### Textos sencillos (147 a 175)

**Texto A · Aviso de empleo (147 a 150)**
> Riverside Cafe is looking for a part-time cashier. Hours are 15 to 20 per week, mainly on weekends. Applicants must be at least 18 years old, be able to work with a point-of-sale system, and speak English and Spanish. Interested candidates should bring a résumé to the cafe between 9 and 11 a.m. on Monday, or email it to jobs@riverside-cafe.example by Friday.

147. How many hours per week is the job? A) 9 to 11 B) 15 to 20 C) 35 D) 18 → **B**
148. Which skill is NOT required? A) Using a point-of-sale system B) Speaking English and Spanish C) Driving D) Being at least 18 → **C**
149. By which day can applicants email a résumé? A) Monday B) Wednesday C) Friday D) The weekend → **C**
150. When should candidates bring a résumé in person? A) Friday, 9 to 11 a.m. B) Monday, 9 to 11 a.m. C) Monday, 3 p.m. D) Weekends → **B**

**Texto B · Memorando (151 a 153)**
> To: Operations team. From: Daniel Ortiz. We have discovered that the new inventory system counts items returned by customers twice. Until IT releases a fix next week, please record returns manually on the paper form in the back office. Do not enter them in the system. When the fix is installed, we will compare the paper records with the system to correct any mistakes.

151. Why was the memo written? A) To announce a new store B) To hire staff C) To report a system problem D) To close the office → **C**
152. What should staff do with returns until the fix? A) Enter them in the system B) Record them on paper C) Ignore them D) Send them to IT → **B**
153. What will happen after the fix? A) Orders will be cancelled B) Staff will be trained C) The paper will be destroyed D) The records will be compared → **D**

**Texto C · Anuncio del centro comunitario (154 a 156)**

Participants will learn basic word processing, email, and internet use. No experience is required. To register, visit the front desk or call 555-0142 before September 30. Space is limited to 15 students per class.

155. How long is the course? A) Four weeks B) Six weeks C) Eight weeks D) Two months → **B**
156. What is the registration deadline? A) October 7 B) September 30 C) Every Tuesday D) There is no deadline → **B**

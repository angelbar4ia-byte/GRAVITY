## Tarea 2 · Leer en la vida diaria (R31 a R40)

**Texto 1 · Aviso de la sala de lectura**
> Reading room notice. Opening hours: 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. on weekdays, and 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. on Saturdays. Phones must be switched to silent. Food is not allowed, but water bottles are welcome. Students must show a valid card at the entrance.

31. What time does the room open on weekdays? A) 6 p.m. B) 10 a.m. C) 9 a.m. → **C**
32. What must students show at the entrance? A) a valid card B) a receipt C) a passport → **A**
33. What is allowed in the room? A) food B) water bottles C) loud music → **B**
34. What happens on Saturdays? A) It is closed all day. B) It opens at 9 a.m. C) It opens from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. → **C**

**Texto 2 · Correo de Daniel al personal**
> From: Daniel. To: Staff. Subject: Office move. Next Monday, the accounts team will move to the third floor. Please pack your desk items by Friday afternoon. Desks will be cleaned over the weekend, so do not leave anything behind. The new printer is on the second floor, near the kitchen.

35. When will the accounts team move? A) on Friday B) on Monday C) on Sunday → **B**
36. What should staff do by Friday afternoon? A) pack their desk items B) leave their keys C) clean the printer → **A**
37. Why should staff not leave anything behind? A) The printer is broken. B) The kitchen is closed. C) Desks will be cleaned over the weekend. → **C**
38. Where is the new printer? A) on the third floor B) near the kitchen on the second floor C) in the basement → **B**
39. Who wrote the message? A) the cleaning company B) Daniel, to the staff C) the printer supplier → **B**
40. What is the purpose of the message? A) to ask for a day off B) to sell office furniture C) to announce a move and give instructions → **C**

## Tarea 3 · Leer un pasaje académico (R41 a R50)

> Recent research suggests that sleep plays an important role in memory. During deep sleep, the brain appears to strengthen connections between newly learned information and existing knowledge. This process may help people remember facts and skills more effectively. Some scientists, however, caution that most studies have been carried out on small groups of volunteers, so the results should be treated with care. Others argue that the timing of study matters as much as the amount of sleep. Students who review material before bed may therefore benefit more than those who study early in the morning and then forget it during the day.

41. What is the main idea of the passage? A) Sleep does not affect memory. B) Sleep may help memory, but more research is needed. C) Students should only study at night. → **B**
42. According to the passage, what happens during deep sleep? A) The brain links new information with existing knowledge. B) The brain stops working. C) People forget everything they learned. → **A**
43. Why do some scientists caution? A) The studies are too expensive. B) Students never sleep. C) Most studies used small groups of volunteers. → **C**
44. What does "caution" mean in the passage? A) warn or advise care B) buy something C) celebrate → **A**
45. Which factor do others say matters? A) the price of books B) the timing of study C) the number of teachers → **B**
46. According to the passage, students who review before bed: A) may forget everything B) never study early C) may benefit more than those who study early in the morning and then forget → **C**
47. The word "appears" is closest in meaning to: A) disappears B) seems C) fails → **B**
48. What can be inferred from the passage? A) Stronger conclusions require more research. B) Sleep is useless. C) Morning study is always best. → **A**
49. What does "this process" refer to? A) the study of sleep B) the volunteers' tests C) the strengthening of connections in the brain → **C**
50. What is the author's attitude toward the research? A) Hostile B) Balanced and cautious C) Humorous → **B**

# Listening (18 minutos en el módulo de ruta)

> **Nota:** el texto se lee en esta etapa. Cuando se apruebe el audio, cada tarea se escucha una vez, como en el examen.

## Tarea 1 · Elegir una respuesta (L1 a L15)

Escucha la frase y elige la mejor respuesta.

**L1.** "Did you remember to bring your passport?" A) I don't have a passport. B) Yes, it's in my bag. C) Passports are expensive. → **B**
**L2.** "What time does the bank open?" A) It's a big bank. B) I banked yesterday. C) At nine, I think. → **C**
**L3.** "Who's coming to the party?" A) Most of our friends. B) Parties are fun. C) I came by bus. → **A**
**L4.** "Do you want to share a taxi?" A) Taxis are yellow. B) Yes, that's a good idea. C) I share everything. → **B**
**L5.** "Has the package arrived yet?" A) Packages are heavy. B) I arrived late. C) No, it should come tomorrow. → **C**
**L6.** "Where did you buy those shoes?" A) At the market on Main Street. B) I shoe the horse. C) They buy shoes every day. → **A**
**L7.** "Would you mind waiting here?" A) I'd mind a lot. B) Not at all, I'll wait. C) Waiting is for the bus. → **B**
**L8.** "How long have you lived here?" A) Lived well. B) Here is a town. C) About five years. → **C**
**L9.** "Can I borrow your pen?" A) Sure, here you are. B) Pens are written. C) I borrowed a book. → **A**
**L10.** "Did the meeting finish on time?" A) Meetings are long. B) No, it ran about twenty minutes over. C) The time is nine. → **B**
**L11.** "You look tired." A) Tiredness is a feeling. B) Look at that. C) I didn't sleep well last night. → **C**
**L12.** "Is the museum free on Sundays?" A) Yes, admission is free after noon. B) Museums are old. C) Sundays are for rest. → **A**
**L13.** "Could you pass the salt?" A) Salt is white. B) Here you go. C) I passed my exam. → **B**
**L14.** "Do you have any plans for the holiday?" A) Holidays are fun. B) Plans are on paper. C) We're going to visit my parents. → **C**
**L15.** "Shall we take the stairs or the lift?" A) Let's take the stairs, it's only two floors. B) The lift is a machine. C) I climb mountains. → **A**

## Tarea 2 · Conversaciones (L16 a L25)

**Conversación 1**
> **W:** Hi, I'd like to change my train ticket to Friday.
> **M:** Sure. Do you want the morning or the afternoon train?
> **W:** The afternoon, around two o'clock. Is there a seat by the window?
> **M:** Yes, but there is a small extra charge for changing the ticket.
> **W:** That's fine. How much is it?
> **M:** Ten pounds. I can take the payment now, or I can refund the ticket if you prefer.
> **W:** Let's pay now, please.

16. Where is the conversation taking place? A) at a bus stop B) at a train ticket office C) at a school → **B**
17. What does the woman want to change? A) the day of her ticket B) her seat number C) her name → **A**
18. What does the woman ask for? A) a refund B) a discount C) a seat by the window → **C**
19. How much is the extra charge? A) ten pounds B) twenty pounds C) nothing → **A**
20. What does the man offer? A) to refund the ticket B) to take the payment now C) to call a taxi → **B**

**Conversación 2**
> **M:** Are you coming to the study group tomorrow?
> **W:** I'd like to, but I have a dentist appointment in the morning.
> **M:** Don't worry. We're meeting at four, not in the morning.
> **W:** Oh good. Which room are we using?
> **M:** Room 12 on the second floor. It was booked for the whole week.
> **W:** Great. Shall I bring my notes on chemistry?
> **M:** Yes, please, and the textbook too, if you have it.

21. Why can't the woman come in the morning? A) She is working. B) She lost her notes. C) She has a dentist appointment. → **C**
22. What time is the study group? A) at nine B) at four C) at noon → **B**
23. Where is the room? A) Room 12 on the second floor B) Room 2 on the first floor C) the library → **A**
24. What subject will they study? A) history B) maths C) chemistry → **C**
25. What does the man ask her to bring? A) her laptop B) the textbook C) her lunch → **B**

## Tarea 3 · Anuncio (L26 a L31)

Thank you for your understanding.

27. Which service can students use instead? A) a taxi B) a private car C) the free bus service from the north gate → **C**
28. How often does that bus leave? A) every ten minutes B) every twenty minutes C) every hour → **B**
29. Where has the bus stop moved? A) the car park entrance B) the main hall C) the library → **A**
30. What does the speaker ask students to do? A) to call a taxi B) to bring their bikes C) to note the change → **C**
31. What does the speaker thank students for? A) their donations B) their understanding C) their photos → **B**

## Tarea 4 · Charlas académicas (L32 a L47)

**Charla 1 · Arrecifes de coral**
> Good afternoon. Today I want to discuss coral reefs. Although they cover less than one percent of the ocean floor, they are home to about a quarter of all marine species. Corals are small animals that live in colonies and build hard skeletons from calcium carbonate. They grow very slowly, sometimes only a few centimetres a year. Rising sea temperatures cause coral to lose the algae that give it colour, a process called bleaching. Many scientists believe that reefs can recover if pollution is reduced and fishing is managed carefully. Local communities can help by protecting reef areas and reducing plastic waste.

32. What is the main topic of the talk? A) The history of ships B) Coral reefs and the threats they face C) Fishing techniques → **B**
33. What do reefs support, according to the speaker? A) very few species B) only large fish C) about a quarter of all marine species → **C**
34. What do corals build? A) nests of algae B) hard skeletons from calcium carbonate C) shells made of glass → **B**
35. How fast do corals grow? A) very slowly, sometimes only a few centimetres a year B) very quickly C) about a metre a year → **A**
36. What causes bleaching? A) low temperatures B) too much fishing C) rising sea temperatures → **C**
37. What do scientists believe reefs need in order to recover? A) less pollution and careful fishing management B) more tourists C) more boats → **A**
38. How can local communities help? A) by building new ships B) by protecting reef areas and reducing plastic waste C) by buying more fish → **B**
39. What is the speaker's attitude toward reefs? A) Concerned but hopeful B) Indifferent C) Amused → **A**

**Charla 2 · Mercados nocturnos**
> Let's talk about night markets in Southeast Asia. These markets open after sunset and sell street food, clothes, and handmade crafts. Originally, many were created so that workers could shop after their daytime jobs. Today they attract tourists as well, which has changed their character. Some vendors say that prices have risen, while others say the extra visitors have helped their businesses grow. Local governments are now trying to balance tourism with the needs of residents, for example by limiting traffic in the historic centre. The main challenge is keeping the markets authentic.

40. When do the markets open? A) early in the morning B) after sunset C) at midday → **B**
41. Why were many night markets created? A) so that workers could shop after their daytime jobs B) to attract tourists C) to sell cars → **A**
42. How have tourists changed the markets? A) They closed them. B) They removed the street food. C) They have changed their character. → **C**
43. What do some vendors say? A) Nobody visits the markets. B) Prices have risen. C) The markets are illegal. → **B**
44. What are local governments doing? A) Building new airports B) Closing all the markets C) Limiting traffic in the historic centre → **C**
45. What is the main challenge, according to the speaker? A) keeping the markets authentic B) building new roads C) making food cheaper → **A**
46. What does "authentic" mean in the talk? A) fake B) genuine, true to their original character C) expensive → **B**
47. What is the speaker's purpose? A) to inform B) to sell tickets C) to criticise the vendors → **A**

## Tarea 2 · Escribir un correo (W11)

## Tarea 3 · Discusión académica (W12)

> **Profesor:** Algunas universidades quieren que todos los estudiantes tomen una clase en un segundo idioma. ¿Crees que debería ser obligatorio? Escribe tu opinión en al menos 100 palabras y responde a una de las dos publicaciones de tus compañeros.
> **Compañera A (Mina):** A second language opens doors to jobs and travel, but it takes years to become fluent, so one class may not be enough.
> **Compañero B (Ahmed):** Making it compulsory might make students resentful. It would be better to offer optional courses with good teachers.

**Criterios:** que tu opinión sea clara; que respondas a un compañero; organización; gramática y vocabulario académico.

# Speaking (8 minutos)

## Tarea 1 · Escuchar y repetir (S1 a S7)

Repite la frase tal como la escuchas. Se califica la pronunciación, el ritmo y la exactitud.

- S1. The library will be closed for repairs until Monday.
- S2. Do you know whether the lecture has been moved to room five?
- S3. We've been living in this neighbourhood for almost ten years.
- S4. He rarely eats breakfast before going to the gym.
- S5. I'm going to apply for the scholarship before the deadline.
- S6. If the forecast is correct, we'll have a picnic on Sunday.
- S7. The new bridge has reduced the traffic in the city centre.

## Tarea 2 · Entrevista (I1 a I4)

Responde cada pregunta con claridad y con detalles. Tienes unos 30 segundos por respuesta.

- I1. Which place in your city would you recommend to a visitor, and why?
- I2. Tell me about a time you had to solve a problem at work or school.
- I3. What is a habit you would like to change, and how would you do it?
- I4. Do you prefer to travel with a group or alone? Why?

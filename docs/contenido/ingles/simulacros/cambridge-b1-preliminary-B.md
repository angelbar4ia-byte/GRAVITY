> **Contexto:** nota general en `../CONTEXTO.md`. Estado: simulacro B de B1 Preliminary, completo en formato. Listening en texto hasta que haya audio; Speaking con imágenes descritas en texto.
> Formato: [B1 Preliminary, manual para profesores (Cambridge English, PDF)](https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/Images/168150-b1-preliminary-teachers-handbook.pdf). Reading: 6 partes, 32 preguntas (P1 cinco textos con 3 opciones; P2 cinco personas con ocho textos; P3 cinco preguntas con 4 opciones; P4 cinco oraciones de siete; P5 seis huecos con 4 opciones; P6 seis huecos con una palabra). # Reading (45 minutos)

## Parte 1 · Preguntas 1 a 5 · Opción múltiple (3 opciones)

**1.** *(Letrero en la biblioteca)*
> The library closes at six p.m. on Fridays. Books must be returned by five p.m. on that day.

What time must books be returned on Friday? A) six p.m. B) seven p.m. C) five p.m. → **C**

**2.** *(Mensaje de texto)*
> Hi Sam, the train is cancelled today, so I'll take the bus. See you at seven, not six!

What has changed about the meeting? A) the time B) the reason C) the place → **A**

**3.** *(Anuncio en una tienda)*
> Free delivery on orders over thirty pounds. Delivery takes three to five working days.

How long does delivery take? A) over a month B) three to five working days C) one day → **B**

**4.** *(Correo de una maestra)*
> Your homework is due on Monday, not Tuesday as I said earlier.

When is the homework due? A) Monday B) Tuesday C) Wednesday → **A**

**5.** *(Aviso en un parque)*
> Dogs must be kept on a lead at all times. Please clean up after your pet.

What must dog owners do? A) bring dogs to the lake B) feed dogs in the café C) keep dogs on a lead → **C**

## Parte 2 · Preguntas 6 a 10 · Relacionar personas con textos (8 textos, 5 personas)

Lee los textos A a H. Relaciona cada persona (6 a 10) con el texto adecuado. Hay tres textos que no se usan.

- **A.** A new café offers free Wi-Fi and a quiet room for studying.
- **B.** The gym has opened a swimming pool for members only.
- **C.** Our hotel has rooms overlooking the sea, and breakfast is included.
- **D.** The bakery will close for two weeks in August for renovations.
- **E.** The cinema now shows films in English with Spanish subtitles.
- **F.** A cycling club meets every Sunday morning in the park.
- **G.** The school is looking for volunteers to help children read.
- **H.** The museum has a new section about the history of trains.

**6.** Ana wants a quiet place where she can study in the afternoon. **→ A**
**7.** Pablo enjoys riding his bike with other people at the weekend. **→ F**
**8.** Carmen wants to watch films in English. **→ E**
**9.** Luis is looking for a holiday near the coast, with breakfast included. **→ C**
**10.** Rosa wants to help children learn to read. **→ G**

## Parte 3 · Preguntas 11 a 15 · Opción múltiple (4 opciones)

> Why I changed my mind about cycling to work
>
> Two years ago, I thought cycling in the city was far too dangerous. I couldn't imagine sharing the roads with buses and lorries. Then my company offered me a bicycle as part of a scheme, so I decided to give it a try. The first few weeks were frightening, but I soon learned the safest routes. Now I wouldn't go back. I arrive at the office feeling awake and relaxed, and I've even saved money on bus fares. My only advice is to start on quiet streets and build up slowly.

**11.** What did the writer think about cycling two years ago?
A) that it was relaxing B) that it was too dangerous C) that it was expensive D) that it was boring
**→ B**

**12.** Why did the writer decide to try cycling?
A) A friend asked him to. B) He wanted to lose weight. C) The buses stopped running. D) The company offered him a bicycle.
**→ D**

**13.** How did the writer feel in the first few weeks?
A) happy B) bored C) frightened D) confident
**→ C**

**14.** What does the writer say about arriving at work now?
A) He is tired. B) He arrives late. C) He feels awake and relaxed. D) He takes the bus.
**→ C**

**15.** What advice does the writer give?
A) Cycle on busy roads from the start. B) Buy an expensive bicycle. C) Avoid cycling in winter. D) Start on quiet streets and build up slowly.
**→ D**

## Parte 4 · Preguntas 16 a 20 · Insertar oraciones (7 oraciones, 5 espacios)

Elige la oración correcta (A a G) para cada espacio. Hay dos oraciones que no se usan.

> Learning to cook **(16)** ___ Many people think you need expensive equipment, but a few good knives and a pan are enough. **(17)** ___ Start with simple recipes, such as soup or pasta, and gradually try more difficult dishes. **(18)** ___ Reading the recipe carefully before you start is important. **(19)** ___ Friends and family can be a great source of advice. **(20)** ___ Most importantly, don't be afraid of mistakes, because even experienced cooks sometimes burn the food.

A. It is also a good idea to plan your shopping in advance.
B. Many successful cooks started by making mistakes in their own kitchen.
C. This means you can save money and still eat well.
D. Remember that every dish takes practice.
E. Some people even enjoy the process more than the meal.
F. It is also helpful to ask experienced people for their tips.
G. Cooking can be a rewarding hobby for anyone.

**16. G** · **17. C** · **18. A** · **19. D** · **20. B**
*(No se usan: E y F.)*

## Parte 5 · Preguntas 21 a 26 · Opción múltiple con vocabulario (4 opciones)

> Dear Mum, I'm writing to tell you about my new flat. It's **(21)** ___ the city centre, so I can walk to work. The kitchen is small, but it has a **(22)** ___ balcony where I can sit in the evening. My flatmates are very friendly; they **(23)** ___ me to dinner last Sunday. I'm still **(24)** ___ to the noise of the trams, but I'm getting used to it. I **(25)** ___ you can visit me soon. Let me know **(26)** ___ you're free next month. Love, Anna

**21.** A) at B) on C) in D) by → **C**
**22.** A) pleasure B) pleasant C) pleasantly D) pleasing → **B**
**23.** A) invite B) invited C) inviting D) invitation → **B**
**24.** A) used B) getting used C) use D) get used → **B**
**25.** A) hope B) hoping C) hopes D) hoped → **A**
**26.** A) that B) because C) if D) what → **C**

## Parte 6 · Preguntas 27 a 32 · Completar con una palabra

Escribe **una** palabra para cada espacio.

> Sophie has just started a new job in a bank. She **(27)** ___ worked in a shop for five years, so the change was big. Her first week was **(28)** ___ hard, because there was so much to learn. However, her colleagues were very helpful, and they showed her **(29)** ___ to use the computer system. Sophie says she **(30)** ___ enjoys the job more than she expected. **(31)** ___ she finishes work, she usually goes to the gym. She hopes **(32)** ___ get a promotion next year.

**27. had** · **28. very** · **29. how** · **30. now** · **31. When** · **32. to**

# Writing (45 minutos)

## Tarea 1 (obligatoria) · Correo · unas 100 palabras

Lee el correo de tu amigo Tom y responde. Tu respuesta debe incluir los cuatro puntos.

> **From:** Tom
> **Subject:** Surprise party!
>
> I'm planning a surprise birthday party for Lisa next Saturday. Could you **(1) help me choose a cake**? Please **(2) let me know if you can bring some music**. **(3) Tell me what time would suit you to arrive**, because I want to plan the timings. And **(4) suggest a good place to hold it**. Thanks!
> Tom

**Criterios:** los cuatro puntos cubiertos (1 punto cada uno); tono amistoso y adecuado; gramática y vocabulario del nivel; unas 100 palabras.

## Tarea 2 (elige una) · unas 100 palabras

**Opción A · Artículo para la revista de la escuela:** escribe un artículo sobre un deporte o actividad que disfrutas. Explica por qué te gusta y qué consejo darías a alguien que quiera empezar.

**Opción B · Historia:** escribe una historia que empiece con esta frase: *"When the phone rang at midnight, I already knew something was wrong."*

**Criterios:** tipo de texto y propósito; extensión; organización; vocabulario y gramática.

# Listening (30 minutos)

> **Nota:** el texto se lee. Cuando se apruebe el audio, cada parte se escucha dos veces.

## Parte 1 · Preguntas 1 a 7 · Opción múltiple con imágenes

*Las imágenes se describen con texto (A, B, C).*

**1.** *¿Dónde están las llaves del coche?*
> Where did you put the car keys? / In the kitchen drawer, next to the phone.
A) in the kitchen drawer B) on the table C) in the car → **A**

**2.** *¿A qué hora deben salir?*
> What time does the film start? / Half past seven. We should leave at seven to get popcorn.
A) six B) seven C) half past seven → **B**

**3.** *¿Cuándo abre el museo?*
> Is the museum open on Mondays? / No, it's closed on Mondays, but it opens on Tuesday at ten.
A) Monday at ten B) Monday at eleven C) Tuesday at ten → **C**

**4.** *¿Dónde está la bolsa?*
> Your bag is on the sofa. / Oh, thanks. I thought I'd left it in the hall.
A) in the hall B) on the sofa C) in the car → **B**

**5.** *¿Qué opinó el hombre del concierto?*
> Did you enjoy the concert? / It was okay, but the music was too loud for my taste.
A) He didn't go. B) It was excellent. C) It was okay. → **C**

**6.** *¿Cuándo va a llover?*
> The forecast says rain this afternoon. / Then let's take umbrellas and go in the morning.
A) in the morning B) in the afternoon C) all day → **B**

**7.** *¿Qué autobús deben tomar?*
> Excuse me, is this the bus to the station? / No, that one goes to the airport. Take the number twelve.
A) the airport bus B) the station bus C) number twelve → **C**

## Parte 2 · Preguntas 8 a 13 · Frases incompletas (una palabra o número)

> Last term our class went on a trip to the national park. We left school at eight o'clock in a large coach. The journey took about two hours, and the teacher had planned several activities. The first was a walk to a waterfall, which was longer than we expected. After lunch, we visited a visitor centre, where a ranger showed us the local animals. The best part was the boat ride on the lake. Everyone was tired on the way home, but they all agreed it was a fantastic day.

**8.** The class left school at ________. **eight o'clock**
**9.** The journey took about ________ hours. **two**
**10.** The first activity was a walk to a ________. **waterfall**
**11.** After lunch, a ________ showed them the local animals. **ranger**
**12.** The best part was the boat ride on the ________. **lake**
**13.** Everyone was tired on the way ________. **home**

## Parte 3 · Preguntas 14 a 19 · Opción múltiple (3 opciones)

> **Man:** How did the interview go?
> **Woman:** Honestly, better than I expected. They asked why I wanted the job, and I explained my experience with customers.
> **Man:** Did they ask anything difficult?
> **Woman:** Yes, they asked about a time I made a mistake. I talked about how I fixed it with a client.
> **Man:** That sounds good. When will you know?
> **Woman:** They said next week, but I'm not sure. Actually, I'm feeling quite nervous about it.

**14.** How did the interview go? A) badly B) better than expected C) the same as usual → **B**
**15.** What did they ask her about? A) her salary B) her holidays C) why she wanted the job → **C**
**16.** What difficult question did they ask? A) about a mistake she made B) about her family C) about her salary → **A**
**17.** How did she answer that question? A) She said she never made mistakes. B) She explained how she fixed a mistake with a client. C) She refused to answer. → **B**
**18.** When will she know the result? A) next week B) next month C) today → **A**
**19.** How does she feel now? A) confident B) nervous C) bored → **B**

## Parte 4 · Preguntas 20 a 25 · Opción múltiple (3 opciones)

> Hello, I'm Jorge, and I've volunteered at the city zoo for five years. I started because I wanted to work with animals, but I didn't know how much I would learn about teamwork. Each morning we check the animals' health and prepare their food. It's physically tiring, but I love seeing the children's faces when they visit. Last year I helped organise a school trip for two hundred students. It was stressful, but everything went smoothly. My advice to new volunteers is to ask questions, even when they seem silly.

**20.** How long has Jorge volunteered at the zoo? A) two years B) five years C) ten years → **B**
**21.** What did he learn that surprised him? A) how to cook for animals B) how to drive a truck C) how much teamwork matters → **C**
**22.** What do the volunteers do each morning? A) check the animals' health and prepare food B) clean the cages only C) take photographs → **A**
**23.** What does Jorge enjoy most? A) the salary B) the uniform C) children's faces when they visit → **C**
**24.** What did Jorge do last year? A) built a new enclosure B) organised a trip for two hundred students C) trained new staff → **B**
**25.** What advice does he give new volunteers? A) avoid asking questions B) work alone C) ask questions, even silly ones → **C**

# Speaking (10 a 12 minutos, en pareja)

> Se practica en voz alta, con grabación, y se califica con la rúbrica de GRAVITY.

**Parte 1 · Conversación general (2 a 3 min)**
- Where do you live, and what do you like about it?
- What do you usually do at the weekend?
- Do you prefer reading or watching films? Why?
- What was the last present you gave someone?

**Parte 2 · Turno largo individual (2 a 3 min, con fotos)**
Describe dos fotos (A: una familia caminando por un sendero de montaña con mochilas; B: un grupo de amigos cocinando juntos en una cocina grande). Compara lo que hacen las personas y di qué actividad prefieres.
*Preparación: 1 minuto. Respuesta: hasta 2 minutos.*

**Parte 3 · Tarea colaborativa (2 a 3 min)**
La escuela quiere organizar un evento para recaudar fondos: una noche de películas, un concurso de talentos o un día deportivo. Decidan juntos cuál conviene más y por qué.

**Parte 4 · Discusión (3 a 4 min)**
- Do you think it's important for children to learn a musical instrument? Why?
- How do people in your country usually celebrate birthdays?
- Is it better to study alone or in a group? Why?

## Parte 1 · Extractos cortos (preguntas 1 a 8)

**1.** *Contexto: una mujer habla con la dependienta de una tienda.*
> Could I exchange these shoes? They're too small, and I haven't worn them outside.

What does the woman want? A) a refund B) an exchange C) a repair → **B**

**2.** *Contexto: un programa de radio sobre una gata perdida.*
> The cat is grey with white paws, and she was last seen near the school. Please call if you see her.

What should listeners do? A) feed the cat B) take the cat to a vet C) call if they see the cat → **C**

**3.** *Contexto: dos amigos comentan una película.*
> I loved the ending, but the middle was far too slow. I agree, although the acting was brilliant.

What do the two speakers agree about? A) the middle was too slow B) the ending was weak C) the cinema was full → **A**

**4.** *Contexto: una guía en un zoológico.*
> The penguins are fed at half past eleven, and visitors are welcome to watch from the viewing platform.

When can visitors watch the penguins being fed? A) at ten B) at half past eleven C) after lunch → **B**

**5.** *Contexto: un gerente habla por teléfono.*
> I'm afraid the meeting will have to be postponed until Thursday. Could you let the team know?

What does the manager ask for? A) to book a room B) to cancel the project C) to tell the team about the change → **C**

**6.** *Contexto: un hombre en una agencia de viajes.*
> The flight to Lisbon is fully booked, but we have seats on the evening flight to Porto, which is only an hour by train.

What is the problem? A) the flight is full B) the train is cancelled C) the price is high → **A**

**7.** *Contexto: una estudiante habla de sus exámenes.*
> I'm glad the exam is in June, because it gives me time to revise properly. My only worry is the speaking test, which I've never done before.

What is the student worried about? A) the written test B) the speaking test C) the timing → **B**

**8.** *Contexto: un médico en la radio.*
> Regular walks, even just twenty minutes a day, can improve your mood and sleep. You don't need expensive equipment.

What does the doctor recommend? A) joining a gym B) buying equipment C) taking regular walks → **C**

## Parte 2 · Charla con frases incompletas (preguntas 9 a 18)

Escucha la charla y completa cada frase con una palabra, número o frase de hasta tres palabras.

> The lighthouse at Cape Harbour has guided ships for more than two hundred years. It was built in 1812 by a local engineer, who designed the tower to withstand strong winds. Originally, the light was powered by oil, but it was converted to electricity in 1930. Today the lighthouse is automated, although a keeper visits every week to check the equipment. The building is now a museum, and visitors can climb the 120 steps to the top. Many people come to watch the sunset from the balcony. Lately the local council has been raising money to restore the original lenses, which are very fragile. Restoration is expected to take about eighteen months.

12. The light was originally powered by ________. **oil**
13. It was converted to electricity in ________. **1930**
14. Today the lighthouse is ________. **automated**
15. A keeper visits every ________. **week**
16. Visitors can climb ________ steps to the top. **120**

## Parte 3 · Emparejar hablantes con opciones (preguntas 19 a 23)

Escucha a cinco personas que hablan de por qué eligieron vivir en su ciudad. Para cada persona (19 a 23), elige la razón de la lista (A a H). Hay tres opciones que no se usan.

**Opciones**
A. it has excellent schools
B. it is close to family
C. it has affordable housing
D. the job market is strong
E. it is quiet and safe
F. it has good public transport
G. they grew up there
H. the climate suits them

**Hablantes**
19. *"I moved here for the schools. My children have had wonderful teachers, and there are great after-school activities too."* → **A**
20. *"My parents live five minutes away, so we see them every weekend. That was the main reason we stayed."* → **B**
21. *"I came for a job, but I stayed because the weather is so much better than back home."* → **H**
22. *"We bought our flat here because it was affordable compared with the city centre."* → **C**
23. *"I was born in this town and left for a while, but I came back because I missed the streets and the people."* → **G**

*Opciones no usadas: D, E, F.*

## Parte 4 · Entrevista (preguntas 24 a 30)

Escucha la entrevista con una entrenadora de perros. Elige la respuesta correcta (A, B o C).

> **Interviewer:** Lara, how did you become a dog trainer?
> **Lara:** I started by volunteering at a rescue shelter. I noticed that many dogs were given up not because they were bad, but because their owners didn't understand them.
> **Interviewer:** What's the most common mistake owners make?
> **Lara:** Expecting instant results. Training takes weeks, and owners often give up too soon. I always tell them that consistency matters more than intensity.
> **Interviewer:** Do you use punishment?
> **Lara:** Never. I focus on rewards, because dogs learn faster when they feel safe. Some people think this is too soft, but the results speak for themselves.
> **Interviewer:** What do you enjoy most?
> **Lara:** Seeing the moment when an owner and a dog finally understand each other. That look is worth everything.

24. How did Lara begin her career? A) by training police dogs B) by working at a vet clinic C) by volunteering at a shelter → **C**
25. What did Lara notice about the dogs that were given up? A) They were dangerous. B) They were untrained by choice. C) Their owners didn't understand them. → **C**
26. What is the most common mistake, according to Lara? A) Expecting instant results B) Using expensive equipment C) Feeding dogs too much → **A**
27. What does Lara say about consistency? A) It is hard to achieve. B) It matters more than intensity. C) It is not necessary. → **B**
28. What is Lara's view of punishment? A) It is sometimes necessary. B) She never uses it. C) Owners should use it more. → **B**
29. Why does Lara prefer rewards? A) They are cheaper. B) Owners prefer them. C) Dogs learn faster when they feel safe. → **C**
30. What does Lara enjoy most? A) Winning competitions B) Seeing owner and dog understand each other C) Earning money → **B**

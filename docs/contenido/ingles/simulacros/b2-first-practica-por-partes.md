# Reading and Use of English

## Sesión 1 · Parte 1 · Cloze de opción múltiple (5 huecos, 4 opciones)

> Bajo el cloze, en las preguntas 1 a 5, elige la palabra correcta (A, B, C o D).

> The city council has announced that the old railway line will become a park. Residents have been invited to **(1)** ___ their views at a public meeting next month. Many people welcome the **(2)** ___ of green space, although some worry about the cost. The council says the project will **(3)** ___ several years, and work is expected to begin in spring. Local shops hope the park will **(4)** ___ more visitors to the area. Organisers have promised that the plans will be **(5)** ___ before any final decision is made.

1. A) express B) state C) tell D) say
2. A) emergence B) addition C) arrival D) production
3. A) occupy B) last C) hold D) grow
4. A) move B) guide C) carry D) bring
5. A) displayed B) exhibited C) presented D) showed

## Sesión 2 · Parte 2 · Cloze con palabra abierta (5 huecos)

> Escribe **una** palabra para cada hueco. No se aceptan contracciones.

> Many adults feel nervous about swimming, but it is never too late to learn. The most important thing is to feel **(1)** ___ in the water. Instructors usually begin with breathing exercises, **(2)** ___ help students gradually learn to float. Some people find it helpful to practise **(3)** ___ a shallow pool before moving to deeper water. Progress can be slow, **(4)** ___ it is steady, and most learners are surprised by how quickly their confidence grows. Once you can swim, **(5)** ___ you'll find that it is a relaxing and healthy hobby.

## Sesión 3 · Parte 3 · Formación de palabras (5 huecos)

> Usa la palabra en mayúsculas al final de cada línea para formar la palabra que completa el hueco.

1. The committee's **(1)** ___ (DECIDE) was disputed by several members.
2. The town's **(2)** ___ (COMMIT) to recycling has grown rapidly.
3. The hotel staff were very **(3)** ___ (HELP) with our requests.
4. A detailed **(4)** ___ (ANALYSE) of the report found several errors.
5. He was **(5)** ___ (IMPRESS) by the quality of the food.

## Sesión 4 · Parte 4 · Transformación con palabra clave (5 frases)

> Completa la segunda frase para que signifique lo mismo que la primera. Usa la palabra clave en MAYÚSCULAS, sin cambiarla. Entre 2 y 5 palabras, incluida la palabra clave.

1. It's a pity I didn't book earlier. **WISH**
   I ___ ___ earlier.
2. The train was late, so we missed the start of the film. **RESULT**
   We missed the start of the film ___ ___ the late train.
3. Nobody had ever told me about the problem. **NEVER**
   I ___ ___ about the problem.
4. You cannot use the gym unless you pay the fee. **WITHOUT**
   You cannot use the gym ___ ___ the fee.
5. I'm sure the parcel will arrive tomorrow. **BOUND**
   The parcel ___ ___ tomorrow.

## Sesión 5 · Parte 5 · Lectura de opción múltiple (5 preguntas, 4 opciones)

> Lee el texto y responde las preguntas.

> When Eva started working at the museum, she expected to spend most of her time behind a desk. Instead, she spends her mornings leading tours for school groups. At first, she found it intimidating to speak in front of so many children, but she soon discovered that their curiosity made the job enjoyable. Her manager has noticed how much more confident she has become, and she has now been asked to design a new family trail through the galleries. Eva admits that the extra responsibility is daunting, yet she is looking forward to it.

1. What did Eva expect her job to involve? A) leading tours B) working with children C) spending time at a desk D) designing trails
2. What did she find difficult at first? A) speaking to large groups of children B) the galleries C) the workload D) the wages
3. What has her manager noticed? A) that she is less confident B) that she is more confident C) that she wants to leave D) that she dislikes children
4. What new task has she been given? A) writing a book B) repairing the galleries C) designing a family trail D) managing the budget
5. How does Eva feel about the new task? A) bored B) daunted but looking forward to it C) relieved D) angry

## Sesión 6 · Parte 6 · Texto con oraciones omitidas (4 huecos, 5 oraciones)

> Elige la oración correcta (A a E) para cada hueco. Sobra una oración.

> Last summer, my friend and I decided to walk the coastal path. **(1)** ___ The first day was mostly flat, which made us overconfident. **(2)** ___ By the afternoon, our legs were aching and we had to rest often. **(3)** ___ We reached the village just before dark, exhausted but happy. **(4)** ___

A. Even so, we would do it again tomorrow.
B. The second day, however, was much harder.
C. We had packed far too little water for the heat.
D. Looking back, the trip taught us more than we expected.
E. It was the first time either of us had walked so far.

## Sesión 7 · Parte 7 · Correspondencia de textos (5 preguntas, textos A a D)

> Para cada pregunta, escribe la letra del texto que contiene la respuesta. Un texto puede usarse más de una vez.

**Texto A**
> I used to spend two hours a day commuting. Now that I work from home, I have time for exercise and my family. The only downside is that I miss the informal chats in the office.

**Texto B**
> Our team works across three time zones, so we rely heavily on written updates. This has made our communication far more precise, though some colleagues feel isolated.

**Texto C**
> I need a separate room to concentrate, and my flat is small. Working from home has been a struggle, but the flexibility has helped me manage my health problems.

**Texto D**
> For me, the office is essential. I thrive on the energy of colleagues, and I find it difficult to switch off when my home is also my workplace.

1. Who says the arrangement has a positive effect on their health?
2. Who misses the informal conversations of the office?
3. Who says communication has become more precise?
4. Who finds it hard to separate work from home life?
5. Who needs a dedicated space to concentrate?

## Sesión 8 · Parte 1 · Extractos cortos (3 preguntas, 3 opciones)

1. **M:** The conference has been moved to the Hilton, I'm afraid. **W:** Oh, no. I've already booked the train to the old venue.
   What is the woman's problem? A) She missed a train. B) She has already bought a train ticket for the old venue. C) The conference was cancelled.
2. **W:** Did you finish the report? **M:** I started it, but the figures didn't add up, so I'm rechecking them.
   What is the man doing? A) rechecking the figures B) presenting the report C) writing the summary
3. **W:** Thanks for the lift. I thought I'd be waiting ages for a bus. **M:** No bother, it's on my way.
   What did the woman expect? A) to walk home B) to pay for a taxi C) to wait a long time for a bus

## Sesión 9 · Parte 2 · Charla con frases incompletas (4 huecos)

> Escucha la charla y completa cada frase con una a tres palabras, o con un número.

> Hello everyone. The new community garden will open on the 14th of May. Volunteers are needed on weekday evenings, from six until eight. There is a small fee of five pounds per year, which covers tools and seeds. Please bring your own gloves.

1. The garden opens on the ________ of May.
2. Volunteers are needed on ________ evenings.
3. The yearly fee is ________ pounds.
4. Visitors should bring their own ________.

## Sesión 10 · Parte 3 · Emparejar hablantes con opciones (3 hablantes, 5 opciones)

> Para cada hablante (1 a 3), elige la razón de la lista (A a E). Hay dos opciones que no se usan.

**Opciones:** A) to meet people · B) for the food · C) to relax · D) for the culture · E) to try new sports

1. "I wanted somewhere I could finally switch off and read, so I picked a quiet cottage by the lake."
2. "We went because my children had been asking to see the old castles. History is their favourite subject."
3. "I'd heard the local cooking was amazing, so I signed up for every cooking class I could find."

## Sesión 11 · Parte 4 · Entrevista (4 preguntas, 3 opciones)

> **Host:** Ana, what made you start your bakery?
> **Ana:** I was made redundant from my office job, and I'd always baked for friends. A friend suggested I sell at the weekend market, and it went from there.
> **Host:** What's the hardest part?
> **Ana:** Getting up at four every morning is tough, but the best bit is the customers who come back every week.
> **Host:** Do you plan to open a second shop?
> **Ana:** Possibly, but I want to keep the quality high first. I'd rather grow slowly than lose what makes us special.

1. Why did Ana start the bakery? A) She wanted a bigger kitchen. B) She lost her office job and enjoyed baking. C) A bank offered her money.
2. What is the hardest part of her work? A) getting up early B) finding customers C) paying suppliers
3. What is Ana's view of opening a second shop? A) She refuses to consider it. B) She has already decided to do it immediately. C) She may do it later, once the quality is secure.
4. What does Ana value most? A) low prices B) keeping the quality that makes the bakery special C) growing fast

# Writing

## Sesión 12 · Parte 1 · Ensayo obligatorio (140 a 190 palabras)

> **Input:** Many cities are introducing "car-free" days, when certain streets are closed to traffic for a whole Sunday. Supporters argue that these days reduce pollution and give families space to walk and play. Shop owners often disagree, fearing that fewer customers will visit on those days.
> **Pregunta:** *Should cities introduce car-free days? Write an essay for your local newspaper giving your opinion, with reasons and examples.*

**Criterios (rúbrica de GRAVITY, 0 a 4):** contenido (responde, opinión clara, considera un punto de vista contrario), organización (introducción, desarrollo, conclusión, conectores), vocabulario (precisión B2) y gramática (estructuras complejas con control).

## Sesión 13 · Parte 2 · Tarea a elegir (140 a 190 palabras)

Elige **una** tarea:
- **Artículo:** para la revista de tu universidad, sobre cómo los estudiantes pueden equilibrar estudios y trabajo. Describe los desafíos y da consejos prácticos.
- **Informe:** para la dirección de tu empresa, con dos propuestas concretas para reducir el uso de papel, sus ventajas y sus dificultades.
- **Correo:** a la coordinadora de un curso de idiomas, con comentarios sobre lo que funciona, lo que cambiarías y por qué.

# Speaking

# Clave y retroalimentación

> Cada respuesta incorrecta tiene una **regla**: anótala y repasa el punto antes de la siguiente sesión.

| Sesión | Respuestas | Regla por error |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 A · 2 B · 3 A · 4 D · 5 C | Un verbo se elige por la colocación que forma con la palabra siguiente: *express views*, *the addition of*, *last several years*, *bring visitors*, *presented before a decision*. Revisa las colocaciones, no solo el significado de una palabra suelta. |
| 2 | 1 comfortable · 2 which · 3 in · 4 but · 5 you'll | Un pronombre relativo (*which*) se elige por lo que retoma. Una conjunción (*but*) se elige por la relación entre ideas: contraste aquí. |
| 3 | 1 decision · 2 commitment · 3 helpful · 4 analysis · 5 impressed | El sufijo cambia la categoría: *-ion* o *-ment* para sustantivos; *-ful* para adjetivos; *-ed* con sentido pasivo en un participio. |
| 4 | 1 wish I had booked · 2 as a result of · 3 had never been told · 4 without paying · 5 is bound to arrive | *Wish* + pasado perfecto para lamentar el pasado. *As a result of* para causa y efecto. *Never* va entre el auxiliar y el participio. *Without* + gerundio. *Be bound to* para una certeza. |
| 5 | 1 C · 2 A · 3 B · 4 C · 5 B | Una pregunta sobre lo que alguien "expected" se responde con el texto, no con la lógica. Revisa la frase exacta que contiene la respuesta. |
| 6 | 1 E · 2 B · 3 C · 4 D (sobra A) | Un hueco se decide por lo que viene antes y después: *however* marca contraste con el día anterior; *looking back* marca un cierre. Lee el texto alrededor del hueco. |
| 7 | 1 C · 2 A · 3 B · 4 D · 5 C | Busca la idea exacta del texto, no un tema parecido. Un texto puede servir para más de una pregunta. |
| 8 | 1 B · 2 A · 3 C | Escucha lo que dice el hablante, no solo la palabra que se repite. "I've already booked" implica un problema con el lugar. |
| 9 | 1 14th · 2 weekday · 3 five · 4 gloves | Responde con la palabra exacta que escuchas; no completes con lo que suponga el sentido. Revisa la gramática de la frase. |
| 10 | 1 C · 2 D · 3 B | Un hablante puede describir la razón sin nombrarla: *switch off* significa relajarse. Las opciones no usadas pueden estar en el texto como distractores. |
| 11 | 1 B · 2 A · 3 C · 4 B | Lo que se pregunta es lo que la persona dice explícitamente. "Possibly" no es una decisión; "rather grow slowly" indica preferencia. |

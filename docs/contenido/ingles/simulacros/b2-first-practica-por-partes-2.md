# Reading and Use of English

## Sesión 16 · Parte 1 · Cloze de opción múltiple

> The local tennis club has changed a great deal in recent years. Membership has **(1)** ___ rapidly, and the committee is now looking for ways to make room for everyone. Coaches **(2)** ___ free lessons to children on Saturday mornings, which has proved very popular. The club is also planning a new indoor court, **(3)** ___ will allow players to train during the winter. Funding for the project is still uncertain, but the members are **(4)** ___ optimistic. Anyone **(5)** ___ wishes to help is welcome to attend the next meeting.

1. A) raised B) grown C) produced D) reached
2. A) offer B) offered C) offering D) offers
3. A) because B) so C) which D) that
4. A) cautious B) cautiously C) cautiousness D) caution
5. A) who B) which C) whose D) what

## Sesión 17 · Parte 2 · Cloze con palabra abierta

> Escribe **una** palabra para cada hueco. No se aceptan contracciones.

> Moving to a new country can be exciting, but it also brings challenges. Most newcomers find that the hardest part is **(1)** ___ friends. Joining a local club or class is a good way **(2)** ___ meet people with similar interests. Learning the language is also important, **(3)** ___ it helps you understand daily life. Many people **(4)** ___ that they feel more at home after the first year. It is normal to feel homesick at times, **(5)** ___ talking to others in the same situation can help.

## Sesión 18 · Parte 3 · Formación de palabras

> Usa la palabra en mayúsculas al final de cada línea para formar la palabra que completa el hueco.

1. The company's **(1)** ___ (EMPLOY) rose sharply last year.
2. The results were **(2)** ___ (SURPRISE) for everyone in the room.
3. **(3)** ___ (FORTUNE), the storm missed the town.
4. Her **(4)** ___ (DEPEND) on her family made the move difficult.
5. The house was **(5)** ___ (FURNISH) with antique tables.

## Sesión 19 · Parte 4 · Transformación con palabra clave

> Completa la segunda frase para que signifique lo mismo que la primera. Usa la palabra clave en MAYÚSCULAS, sin cambiarla. Entre 2 y 5 palabras, incluida la palabra clave.

1. It's possible that the bus will be late. **MIGHT**
   The bus ___ ___ late.
2. The manager said I could leave early. **PERMITTED**
   I ___ ___ early.
3. I have not met a better cook than Sarah. **NEVER**
   I have ___ ___ a better cook than Sarah.
4. The storm stopped our flight from leaving on time. **CAUSED**
   The storm ___ ___ delayed.
5. You'll miss the bus if you don't hurry. **UNLESS**
   You'll miss the bus ___ ___.

## Sesión 20 · Parte 5 · Lectura de opción múltiple

> Lee el texto y responde las preguntas.

> Mariana's grandmother ran a small bakery for forty years. Every morning she woke before dawn to prepare the bread, and customers queued outside even in the rain. When she retired, many people assumed the shop would close. Instead, Mariana decided to reopen it, keeping the original recipes but adding a few new cakes. Her biggest challenge has been balancing respect for tradition with the demands of modern customers, who often expect quick service and card payments. Still, she believes that her grandmother would have approved.

1. How long did the grandmother run the bakery? A) ten years B) twenty years C) forty years D) fifty years
2. What did customers do in the rain? A) They stayed home. B) They queued outside. C) They bought cakes online. D) They closed the shop.
3. What did Mariana decide to do? A) to reopen the bakery B) to sell the building C) to move abroad D) to close the shop
4. What has been Mariana's biggest challenge? A) finding a building B) learning to bake C) balancing tradition with modern demands D) paying for ingredients
5. What does the writer suggest about the grandmother? A) She would not have approved of the changes. B) She would have been proud of the shop. C) She would have sold the shop. D) She had no interest in the shop.

## Sesión 21 · Parte 6 · Texto con oraciones omitidas

> Elige la oración correcta (A a E) para cada hueco. Sobra una oración.

> Our school trip to the mountains was supposed to last three days. **(1)** ___ On the first morning, the bus broke down near the border. **(2)** ___ Within an hour, a replacement bus arrived and we continued. **(3)** ___ By the time we reached the hostel, the sun had almost set. **(4)** ___ Looking at the photographs now, I realise it was one of the best trips we ever had.

A. Nobody had expected a problem so early in the journey.
B. The teachers had planned every detail carefully.
C. It was a surprisingly peaceful place to start an adventure.
D. The delay, however, meant we arrived later than planned.
E. Then we ran into heavy rain on the mountain road.

## Sesión 22 · Parte 7 · Correspondencia de textos

> Para cada pregunta, escribe la letra del texto que contiene la respuesta. Un texto puede usarse más de una vez.

**Texto A**
> I deleted my social media accounts last year and I haven't looked back. I sleep better now, and I finally read books again.

**Texto B**
> For my business, social media is essential. It's how customers find us, although I admit it takes hours each week.

**Texto C**
> I use it mainly to keep in touch with relatives abroad. I'd rather they didn't see every detail of my life, so I only post photographs.

**Texto D**
> I was worried it would make me anxious, but it has helped me discover new hobbies and join groups of people who share my interests.

1. Who has stopped using social media altogether?
2. Who says it takes a lot of time?
3. Who limits the personal information they share?
4. Who says it has had a positive effect on their interests?
5. Who says it has improved their sleep?

## Sesión 23 · Parte 1 · Extractos cortos (3 preguntas, 3 opciones)

   How does the woman want to travel? A) on foot B) by taxi C) by tram

## Sesión 25 · Parte 3 · Emparejar hablantes con opciones (3 hablantes, 5 opciones)

> Para cada hablante (1 a 3), elige la razón de la lista (A a E). Hay dos opciones que no se usan.

**Opciones:** A) it has good public transport · B) it is close to the sea · C) it has affordable housing · D) it has excellent schools · E) it has a strong arts scene

1. "My children are doing really well at the local school, so we chose the area for the teachers."
2. "We were tired of paying so much rent, and here we found a flat we could actually afford."
3. "I'm a painter, and this city has galleries and theatres everywhere. I've never been so inspired."

## Sesión 26 · Parte 4 · Entrevista (4 preguntas, 3 opciones)

> **Host:** Tom, you've been a firefighter for ten years. What's the most rewarding part?
> **Tom:** Helping people on the worst day of their lives. You can't always fix the situation, but you can make it less frightening.
> **Host:** And the hardest?
> **Tom:** The waiting. We train constantly, but most of the time nothing happens, and then suddenly everything does.
> **Host:** Would you recommend the job to young people?
> **Tom:** Absolutely, but only if they're willing to work as a team. Nobody survives a fire alone.

1. What is the most rewarding part of Tom's job? A) earning good money B) helping people in difficult moments C) working with machines
2. What is the hardest part? A) the training B) the equipment C) the waiting between emergencies
3. What does Tom recommend? A) to avoid the job B) to join if they are willing to work as a team C) to join only if they like working alone
4. What does Tom mean by "Nobody survives a fire alone"? A) Teamwork is essential. B) People should avoid fires. C) Firefighters need more training.

# Writing

## Sesión 27 · Parte 1 · Ensayo obligatorio (140 a 190 palabras)

> **Input:** Schools teach many subjects, but few teach practical skills such as cooking, budgeting or repairing things at home. Supporters believe these skills make young people more independent, while critics argue that schools should focus on core academic subjects.
> **Pregunta:** *Should practical life skills be a compulsory part of the school curriculum? Write an essay giving your opinion, with reasons and examples.*

**Criterios (rúbrica de GRAVITY, 0 a 4):** contenido (responde, opinión clara, considera un punto de vista contrario), organización (introducción, desarrollo, conclusión, conectores), vocabulario (precisión B2) y gramática (estructuras complejas con control).

## Sesión 28 · Parte 2 · Tarea a elegir (140 a 190 palabras)

Elige **una** tarea:
- **Artículo:** para la revista de tu comunidad, sobre el valor del voluntariado para los jóvenes. Describe sus beneficios y da consejos para empezar.
- **Informe:** para la dirección de tu empresa, con dos propuestas para mejorar la hora de comida del personal, sus ventajas y sus dificultades.
- **Correo:** a un hotel donde tu estancia no fue como esperabas, con una queja concreta y una solicitud de solución.

# Clave y retroalimentación

> Cada respuesta incorrecta tiene una **regla**: anótala y repasa el punto antes de la siguiente sesión.

| Sesión | Respuestas | Regla por error |
|---|---|---|
| 16 | 1 B · 2 A · 3 C · 4 B · 5 A | Un verbo se elige por la colocación: *has grown rapidly*. Un pronombre relativo tras coma (*which*) retoma la idea anterior; *who* se usa para personas. Un adjetivo que modifica "members are…" necesita adverbio: *cautiously optimistic*. |
| 17 | making · to · because · say · but | El gerundio sigue a expresiones como *find* + objeto o *the hardest part is*. Un infinitivo expresa propósito tras *a good way*. Una conjunción causal (*because*) explica por qué algo es importante. |
| 18 | 1 employment · 2 surprising · 3 fortunately · 4 dependence · 5 furnished | Un sustantivo de número de empleos usa *employment*. Un adjetivo que describe algo que sorprende termina en *-ing*. Un adverbio de una oración introductoria termina en *-ly*. |
| 19 | 1 might be late · 2 was permitted to leave · 3 never met · 4 caused our flight to be · 5 unless you hurry | *Might* expresa posibilidad. *Be permitted to* sustituye *could* en la forma pasiva. *Never* va con presente perfecto. *Cause* + objeto + infinitivo. *Unless* equivale a "si no". |
| 20 | 1 C · 2 B · 3 A · 4 C · 5 B | Una respuesta sobre lo que alguien hizo se busca en la frase exacta del texto, no en lo que sería lógico. |
| 21 | 1 B · 2 A · 3 E · 4 D (sobra C) | Un hueco se decide por la conexión con la oración anterior y la siguiente: *however* marca un contraste; *the first morning* introduce el problema. |
| 22 | 1 A · 2 B · 3 C · 4 D · 5 A | Lee cada texto por la idea exacta; un texto puede servir para más de una pregunta. |
| 23 | 1 B · 2 A · 3 C | Lo que se pregunta es lo que el hablante hará o necesita. "Which should take ten minutes" describe el trabajo pendiente. |
| 24 | 1 3rd · 2 Tuesdays · 3 eighty · 4 workbook | Responde con la palabra exacta que escuchas. Revisa la forma de un número escrito como palabra (*eighty*) frente a una cifra. |
| 25 | 1 D · 2 C · 3 E (no se usan A y B) | Un hablante puede dar la razón sin nombrarla: "chose the area for the teachers" indica escuelas. Las opciones no usadas son distractores. |
| 26 | 1 B · 2 C · 3 B · 4 A | La respuesta correcta es lo que el hablante dice explícitamente. *Nobody survives a fire alone* es una frase que significa trabajo en equipo. |

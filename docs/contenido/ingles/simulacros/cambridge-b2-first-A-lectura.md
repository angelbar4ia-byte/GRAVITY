## Parte 1 · Cloze de opción múltiple (preguntas 1 a 8)

Elige la palabra correcta (A, B, C o D) para cada hueco.

> Every spring, the city hosts a science fair that attracts thousands of visitors. Students **(1)** ___ months preparing their projects, and the judges **(2)** ___ each entry carefully. This year, a team of twelve-year-olds **(3)** ___ a solar-powered water pump that could help farmers in dry regions. Their teacher said the idea had **(4)** ___ from a conversation with her grandfather, who had spent his life **(5)** ___ irrigation systems. The judges were **(6)** ___ by the simplicity of the design. Although the pump was not the most expensive project, it **(7)** ___ the first prize. The team plans to **(8)** ___ their work at a national competition next year.

1. A) spent B) passed C) took D) lasted → **A**
2. A) noticed B) reviewed C) remarked D) advertised → **B**
3. A) argued B) warned C) designed D) claimed → **C**
4. A) grown B) risen C) developed D) come → **D**
5. A) ignoring B) designing C) repairing D) watching → **C**
6. A) impressed B) impressing C) impress D) impression → **A**
7. A) lost B) delayed C) earned D) refused → **C**
8. A) hide B) present C) forget D) cancel → **B**

Children who study a language early **(11)** ___ better pronunciation, but adults can also make progress **(12)** ___ regular practice. The key is consistency. Those who study for just fifteen minutes a day **(13)** ___ progress faster than people who study for hours once a week. Teachers **(14)** ___ agree that motivation matters most. ## Parte 3 · Formación de palabras (preguntas 17 a 24)

Usa la palabra en mayúsculas al final de cada línea para formar la palabra que completa el hueco.

## Parte 4 · Transformación con palabra clave (preguntas 25 a 30)

Completa la segunda oración para que signifique lo mismo que la primera. Usa la palabra clave en MAYÚSCULAS. Entre 2 y 5 palabras, incluida la palabra clave.

25. I haven't seen Mark for two years. **LAST**
    The ___ ___ ___ Mark was two years ago.
    **Respuesta:** last time I saw

26. Anna is more intelligent than her brother. **AS**
    Anna's brother is ___ ___ ___ Anna.
    **Respuesta:** not as intelligent as

27. Please don't smoke here. **MIND**
    Would you ___ ___ ___ here?
    **Respuesta:** mind not smoking

28. The park does not allow dogs. **PERMITTED**
    Dogs are ___ ___ ___ the park.
    **Respuesta:** not permitted in

29. She started learning German in 2018. **HAS**
    She ___ ___ ___ German since 2018.
    **Respuesta:** has been learning

30. I'm sure he will pass the exam. **BOUND**
    He is ___ ___ pass the exam.
    **Respuesta:** bound to

## Parte 5 · Lectura: opción múltiple (preguntas 31 a 36)

Lee el texto y responde las preguntas (A, B, C o D).

> The Riverton Film Festival began in 1998 as a small event held in a school hall. Over the years, it has grown into one of the most respected showcases for independent filmmakers in the region. This year's festival received more than 600 submissions, although only forty were selected. What sets the event apart, according to its director, is its commitment to new voices. Many of the featured directors had never shown their work in public before. Some critics have argued that the festival focuses too heavily on dramatic films, leaving little room for comedy or documentaries. The director, however, insists that the selection committee considers every genre equally. Attendance has also changed. While early festivals relied almost entirely on local audiences, today visitors arrive from across the country, and ticket sales now cover most of the costs.

31. What is the writer mainly doing? A) criticising the festival B) describing how the festival has developed C) comparing film festivals D) advertising a new event → **B**
32. The word "showcases" is closest in meaning to: A) prizes B) events for displaying work C) cinemas D) schools → **B**
33. What does the director say about the festival? A) It accepts only comedies. B) It is run by critics. C) It is committed to new voices. D) It has lost popularity. → **C**
34. What do some critics claim? A) The festival is too expensive. B) The festival favours dramatic films. C) The festival ignores local directors. D) The festival is too short. → **B**
35. What has changed about the audience? A) It is now mostly children. B) It now comes from across the country. C) It is smaller than before. D) It no longer buys tickets. → **B**
36. According to the text, how are most costs covered? A) Government grants B) Sponsorship C) Ticket sales D) Donations → **C**

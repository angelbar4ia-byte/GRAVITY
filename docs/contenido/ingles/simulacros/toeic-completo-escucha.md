# TOEIC completo · Listening (100 preguntas, unos 45 minutos)

> Versión 1.0 (14/nov/2026). Formato: [TOEIC Listening and Reading, handbook de ETS](https://www.ets.org/content/dam/ets-org/pdfs/toeic/toeic-listening-reading-test-examinee-handbook.pdf): Parte 1 (6, fotografías), Parte 2 (25, pregunta-respuesta), Parte 3 (39, conversaciones de 3 preguntas), Parte 4 (30, charlas de 3 preguntas).
> **Preguntas originales de GRAVITY.** No reproducen material de ETS.
> **Limitación en esta etapa:** el audio se presenta como texto. Las fotografías (Parte 1) se describen con texto. Cuando se apruebe el audio y las imágenes, la prueba se escucha y se ve como en el examen.

## Parte 1 · Fotografías (preguntas 1 a 6)

En el examen se escucha una descripción de cada fotografía. Elige la frase que describe la foto.

**1.** *Foto: un hombre trabaja en una computadora portátil en un escritorio.*
A) He is writing by hand. B) He is working on a laptop. C) He is standing up. D) He is painting.
**Respuesta: B**

**2.** *Foto: una mujer camina por una calle mojada con un paraguas abierto.*
A) The street is dry. B) She is carrying an umbrella. C) She is running. D) Children are playing.
**Respuesta: B**

**3.** *Foto: varias personas se sientan alrededor de una mesa en una sala de juntas.*
A) They are eating lunch. B) They are having a meeting. C) They are playing games. D) They are cleaning.
**Respuesta: B**

**4.** *Foto: un perro duerme junto a una cama.*
A) The dog is awake. B) The dog is playing outside. C) The dog is sleeping. D) The dog is eating.
**Respuesta: C**

**5.** *Foto: varias bicicletas estacionadas cerca de una estación de tren.*
A) People are riding bicycles. B) Bicycles are parked. C) A train is leaving. D) The station is empty.
**Respuesta: B**

**6.** *Foto: un hombre corta verduras en una cocina.*
A) He is cooking dinner. B) He is washing a car. C) He is reading a book. D) He is sleeping.
**Respuesta: A**

## Parte 3 · Conversaciones (preguntas 32 a 70)

Escucha cada conversación y responde las tres preguntas (A, B, C o D).

**Conversación 1 (32 a 34)**
> **W:** The team lunch is on Thursday. Can you order the food?
> **M:** Sure. How many people are coming?
> **W:** Eleven, but two are vegetarian.
> **M:** No problem. I'll ask the restaurant for a vegetarian menu.

32. What does the man agree to do? A) Book a room B) Invite guests C) Cook lunch D) Order food → **D**
33. How many people are coming to lunch? A) Nine B) Ten C) Twelve D) Eleven → **D**
34. What will the man ask for? A) A bigger room B) A cheaper price C) A vegetarian menu D) An earlier time → **C**

**Conversación 2 (35 a 37)**
> **M:** Did the shipment arrive from the supplier?
> **W:** Half of it did. The rest is delayed because of the storm.
> **M:** When will we get it?
> **W:** They say Monday, but I'm not sure.
> **M:** Then let's tell the customers now.

35. What is the problem? A) The shipment was damaged. B) The customers complained. C) The supplier closed. D) Part of the shipment is late. → **D**
36. What does the woman say about Monday? A) She is certain. B) It was cancelled. C) She is unsure. D) It is a holiday. → **C**
37. What does the man suggest? A) Waiting. B) Calling the supplier. C) Informing the customers. D) Cancelling the order. → **C**

**Conversación 3 (38 a 40)**
> **W:** Hi, I'm calling about my internet bill. It seems higher than usual.
> **M:** Let me look. Yes, there's an extra charge for the new router.
> **W:** I didn't ask for a new router.
> **M:** It was sent automatically with the upgrade. I can remove the charge if you return it.
> **W:** All right, I'll do that.

38. Why is the woman calling? A) To cancel her service. B) To buy a router. C) To upgrade her plan. D) To ask about a high bill. → **D**
39. What caused the extra charge? A) A late payment. B) A new router. C) A speed problem. D) A holiday fee. → **B**
40. What will the woman do? A) Keep the router. B) Pay the charge. C) Return the router. D) Change companies. → **C**

**Conversación 4 (41 a 43)**
> **M:** The store manager wants to change the opening hours next month.
> **W:** To what?
> **M:** Open at eight instead of nine, and close at eight in the evening.
> **W:** That's good for the morning customers. But staff will need to start earlier.
> **M:** Yes, we'll offer a bonus to people who agree.

41. What is the conversation about? A) New opening hours. B) Staff salaries. C) A store move. D) A new product. → **A**
42. What is the change for the morning? A) Opening earlier. B) Closing earlier. C) No change. D) A new lunch schedule. → **A**
43. How will staff be encouraged? A) With a bonus. B) With a holiday. C) With training. D) With a raise. → **A**

**Conversación 5 (44 a 46)**
> **W:** Are you ready for the presentation?
> **M:** Almost. The slides are done, but I need to check the sound on the projector.
> **W:** Ask Tom, he knows the room well.
> **M:** I already did, and he said the projector is in the storage room.
> **W:** Then let's go check it together.

44. What does the man need to check? A) The slides. B) The seats. C) The lights. D) The sound. → **D**
45. Where is the projector? A) In the storage room. B) In the lobby. C) In the car. D) In Tom's office. → **A**
46. What will they do next? A) Give the presentation. B) Call the manager. C) Print the slides. D) Check the projector together. → **D**

**Conversación 6 (47 a 49)**
> **M:** Excuse me, I'd like to return these shoes.
> **W:** Do you have the receipt?
> **M:** Yes, here it is. But I wore them outside.
> **W:** I'm sorry, we can only accept returns if the shoes are unworn.
> **M:** They're only a little dirty.
> **W:** Then I can offer you a store credit instead.

47. What is the woman's rule for returns? A) Returns need a receipt. B) Returns must be unworn. C) Returns are never allowed. D) Returns take two weeks. → **B**
48. What does the man have? A) The shoes. B) A receipt. C) A bag. D) A coupon. → **B**
49. What does the woman offer? A) A refund. B) A new pair. C) A discount. D) A store credit. → **D**

**Conversación 7 (50 a 52)**
> **W:** The city council has approved the new bike lane.
> **M:** That's great. When will it be built?
> **W:** They start next spring, and it should take about four months.
> **M:** Will it cut into the road for cars?
> **W:** Yes, one lane will be removed on the main avenue.

50. What was approved? A) A new bus route. B) A bike lane. C) A parking lot. D) A park. → **B**
51. When will construction start? A) Next winter. B) Next spring. C) This summer. D) Next month. → **B**
52. What will change for cars? A) A new road. B) Parking will be free. C) Traffic lights will be added. D) One lane will be removed. → **D**

**Conversación 8 (53 a 55)**
> **M:** I'm thinking of joining the gym near my office.
> **W:** Which one? The one on Fifth Street?
> **M:** Yes, it has a pool and classes in the evening.
> **W:** It's expensive, though.
> **M:** True, but I could use the pool after work instead of going home first.

53. What is the man thinking about doing? A) Changing offices. B) Joining a gym. C) Buying a pool. D) Moving house. → **B**
54. What does the woman say about the gym? A) It is cheap. B) It is far away. C) It is closed. D) It is expensive. → **D**
55. Why does the man like the gym? A) It is close to his home. B) He can swim after work. C) It is cheap. D) It has a café. → **B**

**Conversación 9 (56 a 58)**
> **W:** Have you booked the flights for the conference?
> **M:** Yes, we fly on the twelfth. The return is on the fifteenth.
> **W:** Good. And the hotel?
> **M:** I'm waiting for confirmation. They said by tomorrow.

56. When do they fly? A) On the tenth. B) On the fifteenth. C) Tomorrow. D) On the twelfth. → **D**
57. When is the return flight? A) The tenth. B) The twelfth. C) The fifteenth. D) The twentieth. → **C**
58. What is the man waiting for? A) Flight tickets. B) A visa. C) Hotel confirmation. D) The lunch menu. → **C**

**Conversación 10 (59 a 61)**
> **M:** Our team lost the match yesterday.
> **W:** I know, but we played well.
> **M:** We missed several chances, especially in the second half.
> **W:** True, but the coach said we improved our defence.
> **M:** Then next week we'll practise our shooting.

59. What happened yesterday? A) They won. B) They tied. C) It was cancelled. D) They lost. → **D**
60. What does the woman think about the team's play? A) It was poor. B) It was boring. C) It was good. D) It was late. → **C**
61. What will the team practise? A) Defence. B) Passing. C) Shooting. D) Running. → **C**

**Conversación 11 (62 a 64)**
> **W:** I'd like to change my appointment with Doctor Lee.
> **M:** Sure. What day would suit you?
> **W:** Any day next week, but not Wednesday.
> **M:** We have Tuesday at ten or Thursday at two.
> **W:** Tuesday at ten is good.

62. What does the woman want to do? A) Cancel her appointment. B) Change her appointment. C) Ask about a bill. D) See the doctor now. → **B**
63. Which day does the woman NOT want? A) Tuesday. B) Thursday. C) Monday. D) Wednesday. → **D**
64. What time is the new appointment? A) Ten o'clock. B) Two o'clock. C) Nine o'clock. D) Noon. → **A**

**Conversación 12 (65 a 67)**
> **M:** Have you seen the new kitchen layout?
> **W:** Yes, it looks bigger.
> **M:** The sink is in a better place, but the oven is far from the fridge.
> **W:** We can ask the designer to move it.

65. What is the conversation about? A) A new car. B) A kitchen layout. C) A holiday. D) A bank loan. → **B**
66. What does the man think about the sink? A) It is too big. B) It is broken. C) It is too small. D) It is in a better place. → **D**
67. What does the woman suggest? A) Moving the oven. B) Removing the fridge. C) Buying a new sink. D) Ignoring the problem. → **A**

**Conversación 13 (68 a 70)**
> **W:** The survey results are in. Most customers prefer the blue packaging.
> **M:** Really? I thought the green one was more popular.
> **W:** It was, last year. But this time, the blue one got more votes.
> **M:** Then we should change it before the next season.

68. What did the survey find? A) Customers prefer green packaging. B) Customers want no packaging. C) Customers like the price. D) Customers prefer blue packaging. → **D**
69. What was true last year? A) Blue was more popular. B) Both were equal. C) No one voted. D) Green was more popular. → **D**
70. What does the man suggest? A) Keep the packaging. B) Stop selling the product. C) Hire a designer. D) Change the packaging before the next season. → **D**

## Parte 4 · Charlas (preguntas 71 a 100)

Escucha cada charla y responde las tres preguntas.

**Charla 1 (71 a 73)**
> Please remember that the library will be closed on Monday for a staff training day. The library will reopen on Tuesday at nine. Books can be returned at the desk on Monday, but there will be no computer access.

71. Why is the library closed? A) Repairs. B) A storm. C) A holiday. D) Staff training. → **D**
72. When does the library reopen? A) Monday at nine. B) Tuesday at eight. C) Tuesday at nine. D) Wednesday. → **C**
73. What is not available on Monday? A) Books. B) The desk. C) Computer access. D) Returns. → **C**

**Charla 2 (74 a 76)**
> Good afternoon. Our hotel is pleased to announce a new spa service. The spa opens next week and offers massages, facials, and yoga classes. Guests receive a ten percent discount on all services for their first visit.

74. What is announced? A) A new restaurant. B) A new pool. C) A new spa. D) A new bus service. → **C**
75. What services are offered? A) Cooking classes. B) Swimming. C) Tours. D) Massages, facials, and yoga. → **D**
76. What discount is offered? A) Five percent. B) Ten percent. C) Twenty percent. D) None. → **B**

**Charla 3 (77 a 79)**
> Hello, everyone. This afternoon's workshop on time management has been moved to room 204 because of a problem with the projector in room 201. Please bring your laptops. The session begins at three instead of two.

77. What has changed? A) The date. B) The speaker. C) The price. D) The room. → **D**

**Charla 4 (80 a 82)**

**Charla 5 (83 a 85)**

85. What must sales staff do by Friday? A) Pay a fee. B) Submit a report. C) Confirm attendance. D) Call a client. → **C**

**Charla 6 (86 a 88)**

86. What will happen on Thursday? A) A power cut. B) A gas outage. C) A lift repair. D) The water supply will be off. → **D**
87. How long will it be off? A) Two hours. B) Eight hours. C) All day. D) Four hours. → **D**
88. Why? A) A new building. B) Repairs to a pipe. C) Cleaning. D) Painting. → **B**

**Charla 7 (89 a 91)**
> Attention, shoppers. The supermarket on Main Street has a special offer this week: buy two, get one free on all bottled drinks. The offer ends on Sunday at closing time. Please ask the cashier at the register near the entrance.

89. What is the special offer? A) Buy two, get one free. B) Half price. C) Free delivery. D) A discount on bread. → **A**
90. When does the offer end? A) Friday. B) Saturday. C) Monday. D) Sunday. → **D**
91. Where should customers ask? A) At the bakery. B) At the register near the entrance. C) At the desk. D) Online. → **B**

**Charla 8 (92 a 94)**
> Good morning. Our bus route 12 will be diverted this week because of road works on Oak Avenue. Buses will stop at Pine Street instead. Passengers should use the stop on Pine Street, opposite the bank.

92. What is the problem? A) An accident. B) A bus shortage. C) A fare increase. D) Road works. → **D**

**Charla 9 (95 a 97)**

**Charla 10 (98 a 100)**

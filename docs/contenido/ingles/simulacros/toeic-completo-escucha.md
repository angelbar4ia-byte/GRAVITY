## Parte 1 · Fotografías (preguntas 1 a 6)

En el examen se escucha una descripción de cada fotografía. Elige la frase que describe la foto.

**1.** *Foto: un hombre trabaja en una computadora portátil en un escritorio.*
A) He is working on a laptop. B) He is writing by hand. C) He is standing up. D) He is painting.
**Respuesta: B**

**2.** *Foto: una mujer camina por una calle mojada con un paraguas abierto.*
A) The street is dry. B) She is carrying an umbrella. C) She is running. D) Children are playing.
**Respuesta: B**

**3.** *Foto: varias personas se sientan alrededor de una mesa en una sala de juntas.*
A) They are eating lunch. B) They are playing games. C) They are cleaning. D) They are having a meeting.
**Respuesta: B**

**4.** *Foto: un perro duerme junto a una cama.*
A) The dog is awake. B) The dog is sleeping. C) The dog is playing outside. D) The dog is eating.
**Respuesta: C**

**5.** *Foto: varias bicicletas estacionadas cerca de una estación de tren.*
A) People are riding bicycles. B) A train is leaving. C) The station is empty. D) Bicycles are parked.
**Respuesta: B**

**6.** *Foto: un hombre corta verduras en una cocina.*
A) He is cooking dinner. B) He is washing a car. C) He is reading a book. D) He is sleeping.
**Respuesta: A**

## Parte 3 · Conversaciones (preguntas 32 a 70)

Escucha cada conversación y responde las tres preguntas (A, B, C o D).

**Conversación 1 (32 a 34)**
> **W:** The team lunch is on Thursday. Can you order the food?
> **M:** Sure. How many people are coming?
> **W:** Eleven, but two are vegetarian.
> **M:** No problem. I'll ask the restaurant for a vegetarian menu.

32. What does the man agree to do?A) Book a room B) Order food C) Invite guests D) Cook lunch → **B**
33. How many people are coming to lunch?A) Nine B) Ten C) Twelve D) Eleven → **D**
34. What will the man ask for?A) A vegetarian menu B) A bigger room C) A cheaper price D) An earlier time → **A**

**Conversación 2 (35 a 37)**
> **M:** Did the shipment arrive from the supplier?
> **W:** Half of it did. The rest is delayed because of the storm.
> **M:** When will we get it?
> **W:** They say Monday, but I'm not sure.
> **M:** Then let's tell the customers now.

35. What is the problem?A) The shipment was damaged. B) Part of the shipment is late. C) The customers complained. D) The supplier closed. → **B**
36. What does the woman say about Monday?A) She is unsure. B) She is certain. C) It was cancelled. D) It is a holiday. → **A**
37. What does the man suggest?A) Waiting. B) Informing the customers. C) Calling the supplier. D) Cancelling the order. → **B**

**Conversación 3 (38 a 40)**
> **W:** Hi, I'm calling about my internet bill. It seems higher than usual.
> **M:** Let me look. Yes, there's an extra charge for the new router.
> **W:** I didn't ask for a new router.
> **M:** It was sent automatically with the upgrade. I can remove the charge if you return it.
> **W:** All right, I'll do that.

38. Why is the woman calling?A) To cancel her service. B) To buy a router. C) To ask about a high bill. D) To upgrade her plan. → **C**
39. What caused the extra charge?A) A late payment. B) A new router. C) A speed problem. D) A holiday fee. → **B**
40. What will the woman do?A) Return the router. B) Keep the router. C) Pay the charge. D) Change companies. → **A**

**Conversación 4 (41 a 43)**
> **M:** The store manager wants to change the opening hours next month.
> **W:** To what?
> **M:** Open at eight instead of nine, and close at eight in the evening.
> **W:** That's good for the morning customers. But staff will need to start earlier.
> **M:** Yes, we'll offer a bonus to people who agree.

41. What is the conversation about?A) Staff salaries. B) A store move. C) A new product. D) New opening hours. → **D**
42. What is the change for the morning?A) Opening earlier. B) Closing earlier. C) No change. D) A new lunch schedule. → **A**
43. How will staff be encouraged?A) With a holiday. B) With training. C) With a bonus. D) With a raise. → **C**

**Conversación 5 (44 a 46)**
> **W:** Are you ready for the presentation?
> **M:** Almost. The slides are done, but I need to check the sound on the projector.
> **W:** Ask Tom, he knows the room well.
> **M:** I already did, and he said the projector is in the storage room.
> **W:** Then let's go check it together.

44. What does the man need to check?A) The sound. B) The slides. C) The seats. D) The lights. → **A**
45. Where is the projector?A) In the lobby. B) In the storage room. C) In the car. D) In Tom's office. → **B**
46. What will they do next?A) Give the presentation. B) Check the projector together. C) Call the manager. D) Print the slides. → **B**

**Conversación 6 (47 a 49)**
> **M:** Excuse me, I'd like to return these shoes.
> **W:** Do you have the receipt?
> **M:** Yes, here it is. But I wore them outside.
> **W:** I'm sorry, we can only accept returns if the shoes are unworn.
> **M:** They're only a little dirty.
> **W:** Then I can offer you a store credit instead.

47. What is the woman's rule for returns?A) Returns need a receipt. B) Returns are never allowed. C) Returns take two weeks. D) Returns must be unworn. → **D**
48. What does the man have?A) The shoes. B) A bag. C) A coupon. D) A receipt. → **D**
49. What does the woman offer?A) A refund. B) A new pair. C) A store credit. D) A discount. → **C**

**Conversación 7 (50 a 52)**
> **W:** The city council has approved the new bike lane.
> **M:** That's great. When will it be built?
> **W:** They start next spring, and it should take about four months.
> **M:** Will it cut into the road for cars?
> **W:** Yes, one lane will be removed on the main avenue.

50. What was approved?A) A new bus route. B) A parking lot. C) A park. D) A bike lane. → **D**
51. When will construction start?A) Next winter. B) This summer. C) Next month. D) Next spring. → **D**
52. What will change for cars?A) A new road. B) Parking will be free. C) One lane will be removed. D) Traffic lights will be added. → **C**

**Conversación 8 (53 a 55)**

> **W:** Which one? The one on Fifth Street?

> **W:** It's expensive, though.

**Conversación 9 (56 a 58)**

**Conversación 10 (59 a 61)**

**Conversación 11 (62 a 64)**

> **M:** We have Tuesday at ten or Thursday at two.
> **W:** Tuesday at ten is good.

**Conversación 12 (65 a 67)**
> **M:** Have you seen the new kitchen layout?
> **W:** Yes, it looks bigger.
> **M:** The sink is in a better place, but the oven is far from the fridge.
> **W:** We can ask the designer to move it.

**Conversación 13 (68 a 70)**
> **W:** The survey results are in. Most customers prefer the blue packaging.
> **M:** Really? I thought the green one was more popular.
> **W:** It was, last year. But this time, the blue one got more votes.
> **M:** Then we should change it before the next season.

## Parte 1 · Cloze de opción múltiple (preguntas 1 a 8)

## Parte 4 · Transformación con palabra clave (preguntas 25 a 30)

Completa la segunda oración para que signifique lo mismo que la primera. Usa la palabra clave en MAYÚSCULAS. Entre 2 y 5 palabras, incluida la palabra clave.

25. I'm certain that she passed the test. **BOUND**
    She ___ ___ the test.
    **Respuesta:** is bound to have passed

26. Tom regrets not studying harder at school. **IF**
    If only Tom ___ ___ harder at school.
    **Respuesta:** had studied

27. The shop is closed on Sundays. **OPEN**
    The shop is not ___ ___ Sundays.
    **Respuesta:** open on

28. The meeting was cancelled because of the storm. **DUE**
    The meeting ___ ___ the storm.
    **Respuesta:** was cancelled due to

29. They started building the bridge in 2015, and it is still not finished. **UNDER**
    The bridge ___ ___ construction since 2015.
    **Respuesta:** has been under construction

30. Please don't forget to lock the door. **REMEMBER**
    Please ___ ___ the door.
    **Respuesta:** remember to lock

## Parte 5 · Lectura: opción múltiple (preguntas 31 a 36)

Lee el texto y responde las preguntas (A, B, C o D).

> My grandmother never wrote anything down. When I asked her for the recipe for her famous bread, she simply laughed and told me to watch. For weeks, I stood in her kitchen, measuring nothing and asking endless questions. Eventually I understood that the recipe lived in her hands, in the way she tested the dough with a light touch and in the moment she decided it was ready. Last winter, she was too ill to bake, so I made the bread myself, using her method. It wasn't perfect, but she tasted it and nodded slowly. That, I think, was the most valuable thing she ever gave me: the confidence to trust my own judgement.

31. What did the grandmother do when the writer asked for the recipe? A) She wrote it down. B) She laughed and told her to watch. C) She refused to cook. D) She sent a written copy. → **B**
32. What did the writer come to understand? A) The exact measurements were essential. B) The bread needed better flour. C) The recipe lived in her grandmother's hands. D) Bread should be baked faster. → **C**
33. Why did the writer make the bread alone? A) Her grandmother was too ill to bake. B) She wanted to enter a competition. C) The kitchen was closed. D) She had lost the recipe. → **A**
34. How did the grandmother react to the bread? A) She was disappointed. B) She said nothing. C) She tasted it and nodded slowly. D) She asked for another loaf. → **C**
35. What does "judgement" mean in the text? A) a legal decision B) a written rule C) a recipe book D) the ability to make sound decisions → **D**
36. According to the writer, what was the most valuable gift? A) a recipe B) a family photo C) confidence in her own judgement D) a kitchen → **C**

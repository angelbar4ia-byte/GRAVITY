# Reading (45 minutos)

## Parte 1 · Preguntas 1 a 5 · Opción múltiple (3 opciones)

Lee cada texto breve y elige la opción correcta.

**1.** *(Letrero en la alberca)*
> Pool closed for cleaning until 3 p.m. Please use the gym instead.

What does the sign say?
A) The gym is closed today.
B) You cannot swim until 3 p.m.
C) The pool is always open after 3 p.m.
**Respuesta: B**

**2.** *(Mensaje de texto)*
> Sorry, I'm stuck in traffic. I'll be 20 minutes late. Start without me!

What is the writer doing?
A) Asking the reader to wait for a lift.
B) Saying they will arrive late.
C) Telling the reader to leave early.
**Respuesta: B**

**3.** *(Anuncio en una tienda)*
> Summer sale: all jackets half price this week only. Shoes not included.

What is on sale?
A) Shoes
B) Jackets
C) Everything in the shop
**Respuesta: B**

**4.** *(Postal)*
> Dear Sam, We arrived in Lisbon yesterday. The weather is fantastic and the food is delicious. Love, Ana

Where is Ana now?
A) At home, planning a trip to Lisbon.
B) In Lisbon.
C) On a train from Lisbon.
**Respuesta: B**

**5.** *(Etiqueta de un producto)*
> Contains nuts. Keep in a cool, dry place. Best before 12 May.

Why should people be careful with this product?
A) It contains nuts.
B) It must be kept hot.
C) It must be eaten today.
**Respuesta: A**

## Parte 2 · Preguntas 6 a 10 · Relacionar (8 textos, 5 personas)

Lee las ocho descripciones (A a H). Relaciona cada persona (6 a 10) con el texto adecuado. Hay tres textos que no se usan.

- **A.** Cooking class for beginners: learn to make pasta and soup. Weekend courses.
- **B.** Swimming lessons for children aged 5 to 10, in the afternoons.
- **C.** Our bookshop has a new section for travel guides and maps.
- **D.** Gym membership: yoga and dance classes, all levels welcome.
- **E.** Study English with a native teacher. Evening classes, small groups.
- **F.** Garden centre: plants, tools and free advice from our experts.
- **G.** Cinema: family films every Sunday morning, half-price tickets for children.
- **H.** Photography course: learn to take better pictures with your phone.

**6.** Carla wants to improve her English for her job, and she can only study in the evenings. **→ E**
**7.** Tomás loves gardens and wants to buy some plants. **→ F**
**8.** Lucía has two young children who want to learn to swim. **→ B**
**9.** Javier wants to take better photos with his phone. **→ H**
**10.** Elena is planning a trip and wants a guide book to read on the way. **→ C**

## Parte 3 · Preguntas 11 a 15 · Opción múltiple (4 opciones)

> Why I love running in the park
>
> I started running last year because my doctor suggested it. At first I could only manage five minutes without stopping. Now I run for an hour every Saturday with my friend Ben. What I enjoy most is the feeling of freedom when I'm outside in the morning. Some people think running in a city is boring, but I find the park is always different: the seasons change, and there are always new people to see. The only thing I don't like is the cold in winter, although I've bought good clothes for it.

**11.** Why did Maria start running?
A) She wanted to meet new people.
B) Her doctor recommended it.
C) She wanted to enter a race.
D) Her friend asked her to.
**Respuesta: B**

**12.** How long does Maria run on Saturdays?
A) Five minutes
B) An hour
C) All day
D) Half an hour
**Respuesta: B**

**13.** What does Maria enjoy most?
A) Meeting new people
B) The feeling of freedom outside in the morning
C) The cold winter weather
D) Winning races
**Respuesta: B**

**14.** What does Maria say about running in a city?
A) She finds the park is always different.
B) She finds it dangerous.
C) She prefers the gym.
D) She agrees it is boring.
**Respuesta: A**

**15.** What does Maria say about winter?
A) She runs more often in winter.
B) She doesn't like the cold, but she has good clothes for it.
C) She stops running in winter.
D) She goes to the gym in winter.
**Respuesta: B**

## Parte 4 · Preguntas 16 a 20 · Insertar oraciones (7 oraciones, 5 espacios)

Lee el texto. Elige la oración correcta para cada espacio (16 a 20). Hay dos oraciones que no se usan.

> Last year, I spent three weeks volunteering at an animal shelter in Morelia. The work was tiring and often dirty. **(16)** ____ Each morning I cleaned the cages and fed the dogs and cats. **(17)** ____ Some of the animals were very scared when we arrived, but they soon became friendly. **(18)** ____ One dog, called Max, followed me everywhere by the end of the first week. **(19)** ____ The staff were kind and always ready to answer my questions. **(20)** ____ I learned a lot about animal care, and I want to go back next year.

A. It taught me how important patience is.
B. I had never worked with animals before, so I was nervous on the first day.
C. Nobody told me that the hardest part would be saying goodbye.
D. Their behaviour changed completely after a few days of care.
E. The shelter needed help with many daily tasks.
F. We usually finished work before lunch.
G. I would never have guessed how much I would enjoy it.

**16. B** · **17. F** · **18. D** · **19. C** · **20. A**
*(No se usan: E y G.)*

## Parte 5 · Preguntas 21 a 26 · Opción múltiple con vocabulario (4 opciones)

Lee el mensaje y elige la palabra o forma correcta para cada espacio.

> Hi Anna, thanks for your message about the weekend. I'd love to come to the beach with you, but I **(21)** ____ working until Saturday afternoon. Could we go on Sunday instead? The weather **(22)** ____ to be better then. If you **(23)** ____ me your address, I can meet you at the bus station at nine. I **(24)** ____ been to the beach for almost two years, so I'm really looking forward to it. If it's sunny, we could have a picnic **(25)** ____ the beach. **(26)** ____ you bring some sandwiches? I'll bring the drinks. See you soon, Maria

**21.** A) am  B) have  C) will  D) do → **A**
**22.** A) is forecasting  B) is forecast  C) forecasts  D) forecast → **B**
**23.** A) give  B) gave  C) will give  D) gives → **A**
**24.** A) gone  B) been  C) went  D) go → **B**
**25.** A) on  B) at  C) in  D) to → **A**
**26.** A) Can  B) Are  C) Will  D) Do → **A**

*Nota:* la pregunta 26 forma la pregunta *Can you bring some sandwiches?*, por eso la respuesta es **A**.

## Parte 6 · Preguntas 27 a 32 · Completar con una palabra

Escribe **una** palabra para cada espacio.

> Tom has a new job in a hospital. He starts **(27)** ____ at seven in the morning, which is early for him. **(28)** ____ first week was hard, but his colleagues are very friendly. They **(29)** ____ him how to use the computer system, and he learned quickly. Tom says the hardest part **(30)** ____ remembering all the names. **(31)** ____ he gets home, he is usually very tired, but he feels happy. He hopes **(32)** ____ work there for many years.

**27. work** · **28. The** · **29. showed** · **30. is** · **31. When** · **32. to**

# Writing (45 minutos)

## Tarea 1 (obligatoria) · Correo · unas 100 palabras

Lee el correo de tu amiga Sara y responde. Tu respuesta debe incluir los cuatro puntos.

> **From:** Sara
> **Subject:** Surprise party!
>
> I'm organising a surprise party for my brother's birthday next month, and I need your help. Could you **(1) book a restaurant** for ten people? Please **(2) suggest some games** we could play at the party. **(3) Tell me which day is best for you**, because I want to choose the date this week. And **(4) let me know how much money you can spend** on food. Thanks a lot!
> Sara

**Criterios:** los cuatro puntos cubiertos (1 punto cada uno); tono adecuado de correo amistoso; gramática y vocabulario del nivel; unas 100 palabras.

## Tarea 2 (elige una) · unas 100 palabras

**Opción A · Artículo para una revista escolar**
Escribe un artículo describiendo un lugar de tu ciudad que los jóvenes deberían visitar. Explica por qué es interesante y qué se puede hacer allí.

**Opción B · Historia**
Escribe una historia que empiece con esta frase: *"When I opened the door, I couldn't believe what I saw."*

**Criterios:** tipo de texto y propósito (artículo o historia), extensión, organización, vocabulario y gramática.

# Listening (30 minutos)

> **Nota:** el texto de Listening se lee. Cuando se apruebe el audio, cada parte se escucha dos veces, como en el examen.

## Parte 1 · Preguntas 1 a 7 · Opción múltiple con imágenes

*En el examen hay tres imágenes por pregunta. Aquí se describen con texto: A, B y C.*

**1.** *¿Qué pastel va a pedir la niña?* A) pastel de chocolate · B) pastel de zanahoria · C) pastel de queso
> *Mum, can I have the chocolate cake?* / *Not that one, it's too sweet. What about the carrot cake?* / *Okay, that sounds nice.*
**Respuesta: B**

**2.** *¿A qué hora salen?* A) 6:30 · B) 7:00 · C) 7:30
> *The film starts at half past seven.* / *Can we get there earlier?* / *Let's leave at seven.*
**Respuesta: B**

**3.** *¿Dónde está la bolsa?* A) en el asiento · B) debajo del asiento · C) en la cajuela
> *I left my bag in the car.* / *Is it under the seat?* / *No, it's in the boot.*
**Respuesta: C**

**4.** *¿Cómo está el clima ahora?* A) lluvioso · B) soleado · C) nevando
> *It was raining this morning, but now the sun is out.* / *Good, let's go to the park.*
**Respuesta: B**

**5.** *¿En qué salón es la junta?* A) salón 12 · B) salón 21 · C) salón 22
> *The meeting is in room 12, not 21.* / *Thanks, I'll tell Sam.*
**Respuesta: A**

**6.** *¿Cuál es el trabajo del hombre?* A) enfermero · B) farmacéutico · C) médico
> *I work at the hospital, but I'm not a doctor. I help patients with their medicine.*
**Respuesta: B**

**7.** *¿Qué compra la mujer?* A) zapatos · B) chamarra · C) sombrero
> *I wanted new shoes, but they were too expensive, so I bought a jacket instead.*
**Respuesta: B**

## Parte 2 · Preguntas 8 a 13 · Opción múltiple (3 opciones)

> **Ana:** Are you still coming to the concert on Friday?
> **Ben:** Yes, but I can't get there until eight.
> **Ana:** That's late. The concert starts at seven thirty.
> **Ben:** I know, but I'll meet you at the entrance.
> **Ana:** Okay. Do you have the tickets?
> **Ben:** No, you bought them, remember?
> **Ana:** Oh yes, I've got them in my bag.
> **Ben:** Great.
> **Ana:** And bring a jacket. It gets cold after the show.

**8.** ¿Cuál es el problema de Ben? A) No tiene dinero · **B) Llegará tarde** · C) Perdió los boletos → **B**
**9.** ¿A qué hora empieza el concierto? A) 7:00 · **B) 7:30** · C) 8:00 → **B**
**10.** ¿Quién tiene los boletos? A) Ben · **B) Ana** · C) Ninguno → **B**
**11.** ¿Dónde se van a encontrar? A) En la parada del camión · **B) En la entrada** · C) En casa de Ana → **B**
**12.** ¿Qué sugiere Ana? **A) Llevar una chamarra** · B) Comprar comida · C) Tomar un taxi → **A**
**13.** ¿Qué dice Ben de los boletos? A) Él los compró · **B) Ana los compró** · C) Están agotados → **B**

## Parte 4 · Preguntas 20 a 25 · Opción múltiple (3 opciones)

> **Interviewer:** Jen, how did you start your bakery?
> **Jen:** Well, I've always loved baking, but I worked in an office for years. One day I decided to take a course, and that changed everything.
> **Interviewer:** Was it difficult to leave your job?
> **Jen:** Yes, very! My family wasn't sure at first, but they've supported me since day one.
> **Interviewer:** What's the best part of the job?
> **Jen:** Definitely meeting the customers. Many come every morning, and we've become good friends.
> **Interviewer:** And the hardest part?
> **Jen:** Getting up at four o'clock! But I'm used to it now.

**20.** ¿Qué hizo que Jen decidiera abrir la panadería? A) la sugerencia de una amiga · **B) un curso que tomó** · C) una oferta de una empresa → **B**
**21.** ¿Cómo reaccionó su familia al principio? A) estaban contentos · **B) no estaban seguros** · C) estaban enojados → **B**
**22.** ¿Qué le gusta más de su trabajo? A) los amaneceres · **B) conocer a los clientes** · C) cocinar en casa → **B**
**23.** ¿Qué dice Jen de levantarse temprano? A) lo odia · **B) ya se acostumbró** · C) no trabaja en las mañanas → **B**
**24.** ¿Qué hacen los clientes habituales? **A) Vienen todas las mañanas** · B) Le ayudan a hornear · C) Trabajan en la oficina → **A**
**25.** ¿Dónde trabajaba Jen antes? A) en una panadería · **B) en una oficina** · C) en una escuela → **B**

# Speaking (10 a 12 minutos, en pareja)

> En el examen hay un entrevistador y un evaluador. Aquí se practica en voz alta, con grabación, y se califica con la rúbrica de GRAVITY.

**Parte 1 · Conversación general (2 a 3 min)**
- Where are you from?
- What do you usually do in your free time?
- Do you enjoy cooking? Why (not)?
- What was the last thing you bought? Why did you buy it?

**Parte 2 · Turno largo individual (2 a 3 min, con fotos)**
Describe dos fotos (A: una familia cocinando juntos en la cocina; B: personas en un mercado muy concurrido). Compara lo que hacen las personas y di en qué lugar te gustaría más estar.
*Preparación: 1 minuto. Tiempo de respuesta: hasta 2 minutos.*

**Parte 3 · Tarea colaborativa (2 a 3 min)**
Una escuela debe elegir una actividad para el viaje de fin de año: visitar un museo, ir de campamento o visitar una granja. Decidan juntos cuál conviene más y por qué.

**Parte 4 · Discusión (3 a 4 min)**
- Do you think it's important to learn a foreign language? Why?
- How do people in your country usually spend their free time?
- Is it better to travel alone or with friends? Why?

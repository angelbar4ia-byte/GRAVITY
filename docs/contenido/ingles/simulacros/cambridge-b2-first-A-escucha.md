## Parte 1 · Extractos cortos (preguntas 1 a 8)

Escucha cada extracto. Elige la respuesta correcta (A, B o C).

**1.** *Contexto: un hombre habla con su vecina.*
> I'm sorry about the noise last night. We were moving furniture, and it took much longer than we expected.

What is the man doing? A) apologising for the noise B) asking for help C) inviting his neighbour round → **A**

**2.** *Contexto: un anuncio en un tren.*
> Ladies and gentlemen, the next station is closed for maintenance. Passengers should change at the junction.

What is the announcement about? A) a delay B) a station closure C) a change of ticket → **B**

**3.** *Contexto: dos compañeros de trabajo.*
> Have you finished the budget yet? Almost. I just need the final figures from accounts.

What does the woman need? A) the final figures B) more time C) a new budget → **A**

**4.** *Contexto: una maestra habla con su clase.*
> Remember that the exam isn't about speed. Read each question twice, then answer.

What advice does the teacher give? A) work faster B) study at night C) check each question carefully → **C**

**5.** *Contexto: un pronóstico del clima.*
> Tomorrow will be cloudy in the morning, with clearer skies after noon.

When will the sky be clearer? A) in the morning B) in the afternoon C) in the evening → **B**

**6.** *Contexto: un hombre llama por teléfono.*
> I'd like to change my dentist appointment to Thursday, if that's possible.

What does he want to do? A) reschedule an appointment B) cancel a visit C) pay a bill → **A**

**7.** *Contexto: una guía de museo.*
> This painting was completed in 1890, but it wasn't shown to the public until 1920.

When was the painting first shown to the public? A) 1890 B) 1900 C) 1920 → **C**

**8.** *Contexto: un locutor de radio presenta a su invitada.*
> Our next guest has spent ten years researching ocean life, and she's here to talk about her latest book.

What is the guest's job? A) author and researcher B) journalist C) teacher → **A**

## Parte 2 · Charla con frases incompletas (preguntas 9 a 18)

Escucha la charla y completa cada frase con una palabra o frase de hasta tres palabras.

> Today I'd like to talk about urban beekeeping. Over the last decade, interest in keeping bees in cities has grown considerably. Rooftops and balconies are now common locations for hives, and many participants find the activity relaxing. However, beekeeping in a city requires careful planning. Hives must be placed where neighbours will not be disturbed, and beekeepers need to register with the local authority. Another important point is the choice of plants. Urban gardens with flowers throughout the season give bees enough food, which is essential for healthy colonies. Finally, a practical tip: check hives every two weeks during spring, when the colonies grow fastest.

9. Interest in urban beekeeping has grown considerably over the last ________. **decade**
10. Many participants find the activity ________. **relaxing**
11. Common locations for hives include rooftops and ________. **balconies**
12. Beekeepers must ________ with the local authority. **register**
13. Hives must not disturb their ________. **neighbours**
14. Flowers that bloom ________ the season provide food. **throughout**
15. Food is essential for ________ colonies. **healthy**
16. The speaker's final point concerns the ________ of plants. **choice**
17. Hives should be checked every ________ weeks during spring. **two**
18. Colonies grow fastest in ________. **spring**

## Parte 3 · Emparejar hablantes con opciones (preguntas 19 a 23)

Escucha a cinco personas que hablan de por qué empezaron a tocar un instrumento. Para cada persona (19 a 23), elige la razón de la lista (A a H). Hay tres opciones que no se usan.

**Opciones**
A. to relax after work
B. to improve their memory
C. to play music with their children
D. to join a band
E. to return to a hobby from childhood
F. to prepare for a concert
G. because a friend recommended it
H. to meet new people

**Hablantes**
19. *"I started because my daughter wanted to learn the violin, so I decided to join her."* → **C**
20. *"I'd always loved the guitar as a child, but I stopped playing at university. Last year I picked it up again."* → **E**
21. *"My closest friend had been going to the class for months and kept telling me to come along."* → **G**
22. *"After a stressful day at the office, playing the piano is the only thing that helps me switch off."* → **A**
23. *"I wanted to remember song lyrics in a foreign language, so I signed up for a music course."* → **B**

## Parte 4 · Entrevista (preguntas 24 a 30)

Escucha la entrevista con una restauradora de cuadros. Elige la respuesta correcta (A, B o C).

> **Interviewer:** Dr Patel, you restore old paintings. How did you become interested in that?
> **Patel:** When I was a student, I helped in a small workshop that repaired paintings for a local church. I was fascinated by how much history could be recovered from a damaged surface.
> **Interviewer:** Is the work very slow?
> **Patel:** Extremely. A single painting can take months. We never rush, because a wrong move can destroy something that can't be replaced. People imagine dramatic moments, but most of the work is patient, careful observation.
> **Interviewer:** What's the most challenging part?
> **Patel:** Deciding how much to restore. Our aim is to make a painting readable, not to make it look new. Sometimes the marks of age are part of its story.
> **Interviewer:** Do you work alone?
> **Patel:** Usually in a team. We have chemists, photographers and historians. Each person checks the others' decisions, which I think is essential.

24. How did Dr Patel first become interested in restoration? A) by visiting museums B) by studying chemistry C) by helping in a workshop → **C**
25. What does she say about the speed of the work? A) It is extremely slow. B) It is quicker than people think. C) It depends on the size of the painting. → **A**
26. Why does the team avoid rushing? A) A mistake could be irreversible. B) The work is expensive. C) The team is slow. → **A**
27. What is the most challenging part of her job? A) Deciding how far to restore. B) Finding the right paints. C) Working with photographers. → **A**
28. What is the aim of the restoration? A) to make paintings look new B) to make paintings readable C) to sell the paintings → **B**
29. Why does she keep the marks of age? A) They are cheaper to keep. B) Visitors demand it. C) They can be part of a painting's story. → **C**
30. How does her team work? A) Each person works alone under supervision. B) Each member checks the others' decisions. C) They compete for credit. → **B**

# TOEIC · Práctica por partes (Listening 1 a 4 y Reading 5 a 7)

> **Contexto:** nota general en `CONTEXTO.md`. Cobertura en `cobertura-practica.md`. Formato completo en `toeic-completo-escucha.md` y `toeic-completo-lectura.md`; Speaking y Writing en `toeic-completo-hablar-escribir.md`.
> Formato: [TOEIC, handbook (ETS)](https://www.ets.org/toeic/test-takers/listening-reading.html). **Preguntas originales de GRAVITY**; no reproducen material de ETS.
> **Limitación:** Listening se presenta en texto (guion y descripción de fotos) hasta que se apruebe el audio.

## Elige tu nivel de inicio

Tres niveles de dificultad, cada uno con las siete partes. La correspondencia con el Marco Común Europeo es aproximada: **Básico ≈ A2**, **Intermedio ≈ B1**, **Avanzado ≈ B2**.

| Nivel | Cuándo empezar aquí |
|---|---|
| **Básico** | Si la prueba de nivel te ubicó en A2 o si te cuesta seguir frases completas en audio o en lectura. |
| **Intermedio** | Si la prueba de nivel te ubicó en B1 o si entiendes los textos de oficina en general. |
| **Avanzado** | Si la prueba te ubicó en B1+ o B2, o si ya resuelves el simulacro completo con facilidad. |

**Cómo decidir:** haz la parte 5 (oraciones) de cada nivel. Si llegas a 80 % (3 de 4 por cada nivel), empieza en el nivel siguiente. Si no, empieza en el nivel donde llegaste a 80 %. El nivel A1 se cubre con el repaso del programa, que aún no está redactado.

**Umbral:** 80 % por parte y nivel. Si una parte queda debajo, se repite en la semana siguiente con el mismo nivel.

## Parte 1 · Fotos (descritas en texto)

**Básico**
1. *Foto: una mujer escribe en una computadora en una oficina.* A) She is talking on the phone. B) She is writing on a computer. C) She is eating lunch. D) She is painting a wall.
2. *Foto: dos hombres se dan la mano en una sala de reuniones.* A) They are cooking. B) They are running. C) They are shaking hands. D) They are sleeping.
3. *Foto: un camión cargado de cajas en un muelle.* A) A bus is leaving. B) People are swimming. C) A train is arriving. D) A truck is loaded with boxes.

**Intermedio**
4. *Foto: un técnico revisa un motor abierto en un taller.* A) The engine is being repaired. B) The car is being sold. C) The garage is closed. D) The mechanic is on holiday.
5. *Foto: estantes de libros en una biblioteca; una persona busca un libro.* A) Some shelves are empty. B) The library is closing. C) Nobody is in the room. D) A person is searching for a book.
6. *Foto: una mesa de café con dos tazas y un plato de pan.* A) The cups are full. B) The cups are empty. C) The table is being cleaned. D) The bread is fresh.

**Avanzado**
7. *Foto: trabajadores instalan paneles solares en un techo.* A) A roof is being demolished. B) Solar panels are being sold in a shop. C) Workers are cleaning windows. D) Workers are installing solar panels on a roof.
8. *Foto: una enfermera ajusta el suero de un paciente en una cama.* A) The patient is leaving the hospital. B) The bed is being moved. C) A nurse is adjusting an IV drip. D) A doctor is taking a photograph.
9. *Foto: un grupo de ciclistas se detiene en un cruce con semáforo en rojo.* A) The cyclists are racing. B) The road is empty. C) The cyclists are waiting at a red light. D) The bikes are for sale.

## Parte 2 · Preguntas y respuestas

**Básico**
1. Where is the post office? A) It's on Main Street. B) Yes, I posted it. C) At nine o'clock.
2. Who is coming to the meeting? A) The meeting is long. B) In the conference room. C) Ana and Luis.
3. Do you have an extra pen? A) I'm not sure. B) Pens are cheap. C) Yes, in my bag.

**Intermedio**
4. When will the report be ready? A) It was a long report. B) By Friday afternoon. C) In the printer.
5. Why didn't you call me? A) I call every day. B) My phone battery died. C) It's a long call.
6. Would you like to see the new office? A) I'd like that, thanks. B) The office is new. C) Offices are big.

**Avanzado**
7. Could you review the contract before we sign? A) Contracts are long. B) I'll go through it this afternoon. C) We signed last year.
8. Didn't the supplier promise an earlier delivery? A) The supplier is a company. B) Deliveries are usually early. C) Yes, but they've postponed it again.
9. Who approved the budget changes? A) The budget is large. B) The finance director did, last week. C) Changes are common.

## Parte 3 · Conversaciones

**Básico**
> **W:** Hi, I'd like to book a table for two tonight.
> **M:** Sure. What time?
> **W:** Around seven.
> **M:** We have a table at seven thirty. Is that okay?
> **W:** Yes, that's fine.

1. What does the woman want to book? A) a room B) a flight C) a train ticket D) a table
2. What time is the table? A) seven B) eight C) seven thirty D) six o'clock
3. What does the woman say? A) She can't come. B) She agrees. C) She wants another time. D) She has already paid.

**Intermedio**
> **M:** The project deadline has moved to next Wednesday.
> **W:** That gives us two extra days, but the client wants a draft by Monday.
> **M:** Then we need to split the tasks. Can you handle the budget section?
> **W:** Yes, I'll finish it by Friday.

4. What has changed? A) the deadline B) the budget C) the client D) the venue
5. What does the client want by Monday? A) a final report B) a draft C) a meeting D) a signed contract
6. When will the woman finish the budget section? A) Monday B) Wednesday C) Friday D) Thursday

**Avanzado**
> **W:** Thanks for the presentation. The figures were impressive, but I'm concerned about the assumptions behind the forecast.
> **M:** That's fair. We based the growth rate on last year's figures, which may have been unusually high.
> **W:** Would it be possible to run a second scenario with a lower rate?
> **M:** Certainly, though it would delay the board meeting by a week.

7. What is the woman concerned about? A) the assumptions behind the forecast B) the cost C) the length of the presentation D) the budget for next year
8. What did the man use for the growth rate? A) last year's figures B) a survey C) the client's estimate D) a government report
9. What is the drawback of a second scenario? A) It is too expensive. B) It needs new staff. C) It takes too long to plan. D) It would delay the board meeting.

## Parte 4 · Charlas

**Básico**
> Hello, everyone. Our office will be closed next Friday for building work. Please finish your urgent emails on Thursday afternoon. The main entrance will be open, but the car park will be closed.

1. Which day will the office be closed? A) Thursday B) Monday C) Tuesday D) Friday
2. What should staff do on Thursday? A) go home early B) finish urgent emails C) prepare the car park D) send their reports to the manager
3. What will be closed? A) the main entrance B) the kitchen C) the car park D) the main hall

**Intermedio**
> Good morning, team. This quarter we have increased our sales by twelve percent, mainly because of the new online catalogue. However, returns have also gone up, so the quality team will review our packaging next month. I'd like everyone to share feedback with them by the end of the week.

4. By how much did sales increase? A) twenty percent B) twelve percent C) two percent D) twenty-one percent
5. What caused the increase? A) the new online catalogue B) a price cut C) a new office D) a new discount policy
6. What will the quality team review? A) prices B) staff C) training D) packaging

**Avanzado**
> Thank you for joining this session on supplier risk. Diversifying suppliers reduces the chance that a single failure halts production. But diversification has costs: contracts become harder to manage, and quality standards can vary. In my experience, the most effective approach combines a small number of strategic partners with a reserve list of qualified alternatives.

7. What is the main benefit of diversifying suppliers? A) less risk of production stopping B) lower contract costs C) simpler management D) more contracts with the same supplier
8. What drawback does the speaker mention? A) Contracts are harder to manage. B) Suppliers are unreliable. C) Prices always rise. D) Quality standards are always the same.
9. What does the speaker recommend? A) using only one supplier B) switching suppliers every year C) a few strategic partners plus a reserve list D) keeping no backup suppliers

## Parte 6 · Completar un texto (elige la palabra o frase para cada hueco)

**Básico**
> Dear Mr. Lee, Thank you for your **(1)** ___. The package arrived on Monday, **(2)** ___ everything was in good condition. Please **(3)** ___ us know if you need anything else. We look forward to **(4)** ___ you again soon. Best regards, Sarah

1. A) order B) orders C) ordered D) ordering
2. A) but B) and C) or D) so
3. A) letting B) to let C) lets D) let
4. A) see B) seen C) seeing D) saw

**Intermedio**
> To: All staff. Subject: New expense policy. Starting next month, all travel expenses must be submitted **(1)** ___ two weeks of the trip. Receipts **(2)** ___ be scanned and uploaded to the finance portal. Managers will review requests **(3)** ___ Friday. Staff who fail to comply may **(4)** ___ their reimbursement.

1. A) within B) until C) since D) after
2. A) might B) should C) would D) must
3. A) at B) since C) until D) by
4. A) borrow B) lend C) lose D) save

**Avanzado**
> Mrs. Patel, the board has **(1)** ___ the proposal, subject to revisions. The main concern is the cost estimate, **(2)** ___ is based on outdated figures. Your team **(3)** ___ to submit a revised version by the end of the month. In the meantime, **(4)** ___ for the feedback will be essential.

1. A) approved B) approving C) approve D) approval
2. A) which is B) that is C) what D) who
3. A) expecting B) is expected C) expects D) expect
4. A) to prepare B) prepare C) preparing D) prepared

## Parte 7 · Comprensión de lectura (elige la respuesta correcta)

**Básico**
> **Office Supplies Order.** Please write your name and department on the order form. Orders placed before 3 p.m. will arrive the next day. Paper is in stock, but printer ink must be ordered at least five days in advance.

1. Where is this notice from? A) a bank B) an office C) a school D) a hospital
2. When must printer ink be ordered? A) at least five days before B) on the same day C) after 3 p.m. D) on the day of delivery
3. When do orders placed before 3 p.m. arrive? A) the next day B) the same day C) two days later D) one week later

**Intermedio**
> **To: Our customers.** Our shop will move to a new address on 10 June. We will be closed on 9 and 10 June. Gift cards bought before 9 June can still be used at the new location, but they cannot be exchanged for cash. Thank you for your loyalty.

1. When will the shop be closed? A) 10 June only B) 9 and 10 June C) 1 June D) 1 June to 10 June
2. Can gift cards be exchanged for cash? A) no B) yes, always C) only at the new location D) only before 9 June
3. Why was the message sent? A) to announce a sale B) to ask for feedback C) to announce a move D) to ask for a refund

**Avanzado**
> **Quarterly review.** Operating costs fell by eight percent, largely due to renegotiated leases. Revenue, however, remained flat because the expected launch in the Asian market was postponed to the next fiscal year. The board has therefore approved a cost-containment plan while continuing to fund research for the launch.

1. What caused the fall in operating costs? A) lower revenue B) a new factory C) a new factory opening D) renegotiated leases
2. Why did revenue stay flat? A) costs rose B) leases expired C) the launch was cancelled by the board D) a market launch was postponed
3. What has the board decided? A) to cancel research B) to approve a cost-containment plan C) to sell the company D) to close the Asian office

# Clave y retroalimentación

| Parte | Básico | Intermedio | Avanzado | Regla por error |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · Fotos | 1 B · 2 C · 3 D | 4 A · 5 D · 6 B | 7 D · 8 C · 9 C | Describe la acción que se ve, con el verbo en presente progresivo. Descarta opciones que describen objetos que no aparecen en la foto. |
| 2 · Preguntas y respuestas | 1 A · 2 C · 3 C | 4 B · 5 B · 6 A | 7 B · 8 C · 9 B | Una respuesta correcta responde a la pregunta (lugar, persona, momento, motivo). Una respuesta que repite una palabra de la pregunta suele ser incorrecta. |
| 3 · Conversaciones | 1 D · 2 C · 3 B | 4 A · 5 B · 6 C | 7 A · 8 A · 9 D | Un dato exacto (hora, fecha, cantidad) se confirma en la frase del hablante; no lo deduzcas. |
| 4 · Charlas | 1 D · 2 B · 3 C | 4 B · 5 A · 6 D | 7 A · 8 A · 9 C | El orador anuncia la información al principio o al final. Lee las opciones y busca la palabra clave en el texto. |
| 5 · Oraciones | 1 D · 2 B · 3 D · 4 A | 5 C · 6 B · 7 C · 8 A | 9 B · 10 A · 11 D · 12 A | Revisa el tiempo y la concordancia: *wish* + pasado; *has + participio*; *if* + presente en la condicional; *not only* + inversión. |
| 6 · Texto | 1 A · 2 B · 3 D · 4 C | 1 A · 2 D · 3 D · 4 C | 1 A · 2 A · 3 B · 4 C | Un hueco se decide por el significado de la oración completa y el texto alrededor. Revisa los conectores (*and*, *but*, *so*). |
| 7 · Lectura | 1 B · 2 A · 3 A | 1 B · 2 A · 3 C | 1 D · 2 D · 3 B | La respuesta correcta aparece en el texto con otras palabras. Un dato sobre el futuro (*will*) se busca en la frase con esa forma. |

> **Nota de la clave de Parte 1:** las respuestas del nivel intermedio son 4 A, 5 B y 6 D; las opciones se reordenaron para evitar repeticiones.

- Un nivel completo se considera dominado cuando las siete partes llegan a 80 %.
